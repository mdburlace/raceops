**In the Workshop → Car maintenance** has two tabs.

## Checklists

Your car maintenance checklist at three levels: **Weekly**, **Every 2nd race** and **Monthly / 4th race**. A longer level includes the shorter ones.

1. Press **+ Weekly**, **+ Every 2nd race** or **+ Monthly / 4th race**, and pick the car.
2. Tick each item. Some record a measurement (e.g. toe-out or pad thickness) or a note.
3. Press **Save**. It's kept with who did it and when.

## Schedules

**New schedule** sets up a job that repeats on a count, for example:

- *Rebuild LF shock every 8 races*
- *Freshen engine every 600 laps*
- *Bleed brakes every 30 days*

Races and laps are counted automatically from setup sheets for that car or asset. When the job is done, press **Done today** to reset the count.

Due and overdue schedules show under **Needs attention** on Home and the Workshop hub. Schedules for a single asset can also be added from that asset's page.
