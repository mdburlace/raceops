Deleted something by mistake? **Recently deleted** (in the sidebar, under **Profile & device**) lists what's been deleted, and puts it back.

## Put something back

1. Open **Recently deleted**.
2. Find the item. Each row shows what it is, when it was deleted and by whom, and when it will be removed for good.
3. Press **Recover** on the row. To recover several, tick them and press **Recover** at the top.

The item is back straight away, on every device, the next time it syncs. Its change history shows the recovery.

## Who can recover what

| Who | Can recover |
|---|---|
| Everyone | What they made or deleted themselves, if their role can edit that kind of record |
| The owner, or a role with **Recover anyone's deleted items** | Anything deleted in the team. **Just mine** narrows the list to their own. |
| Platform admins | Anything deleted in any team, and in the shared directory (**Platform admin → Recently deleted**) |

If you can't recover something, ask your team manager.

## How long deleted items are kept

The page says how long: 90 days unless your platform admin has set something else. After that, the item is removed for good, with its photos, files and change history. Until then it stays in **Recently deleted**.

Team managers can press **Ask for a different time**, choose how long, and say why. A platform admin decides, and the team is emailed (when email is set up).

To get back something older, or a whole night's work at once, restore a [backup](/help/backups-team) instead.

## Platform admins

**Platform admin → Recently deleted**:

- **Deleted items** lists what's been deleted. Choose **Every team**, the **Shared directory** or one team. Tick items and press **Recover**.
- **How long deleted items are kept** sets the default for every team: a number of days, or **Until recovered** (never removed).
- Each team can have its own time, in the table below. Choose **Platform default** to go back to the default.
- A team's request shows at the top with **Approve** and **Decline**.

Items are removed each hour once they're past their team's time.

**Who can do this:** everyone recovers their own; team managers recover anything in the team; platform admins recover anything and set how long items are kept.
