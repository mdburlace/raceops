You set the team up and keep it running. The owner role can do everything in the team, within the team's licence. A **team manager** is anyone you give one or more team admin rights.

## First week

Follow [Set up a new team](/help/getting-started). The checklist on Home tracks it. In short:

1. [Choose your team type](/help/team-type).
2. [Invite your crew](/help/members-invites) with the right [roles](/help/roles-permissions).
3. [Add your cars](/help/cars), including gear list, ring & pinion and minimum fuel.
4. [Link your tracks](/help/tracks) and [add your assets](/help/assets).
5. [Set up service schedules](/help/maintenance).

Engine shops: also [set rates, tax and terms](/help/shop-settings).

## Ongoing

- **People.** Invite new crew, change roles, remove people who've left. **Team → Members & invites.**
- **Who sees what.** Adjust roles when someone's job changes, e.g. let a driver edit feedback. **Team → Roles & permissions.**
- **Forms.** Add the fields your team actually uses. [Change forms](/help/forms).
- **Licence.** The Licence card shows plan, seats and end date. Renew before the end date, or the team goes read-only.
- **AI.** Watch the monthly spend on the AI card, and turn it off for the team if needed. [AI assistant](/help/ai-assistant).
- **Engines from your builder.** Decide, per engine, whether your laps count on the builder's service tracker. [Engines from your engine builder](/help/linked-engines).
- **Reports.** Results by car, spend and maintenance done. [Reports](/help/reports).
