**Team → Reports** shows:

For the year you pick:

- **Results by car:** events, features, wins, top 5s, average start, finish and places gained, and laps. Download it as CSV.
- **Spend by category:** from the purchase date and cost on your assets.
- **Maintenance completed:** checklists done.

**Team → Crew Chief stats** shows the same results for each crew chief, taken from the *Crew Chief* field on setup sheets.

For charts and trends (results over time, what worked, jetting, service forecasts) see [Analytics](/help/analytics).

Reports read what's already recorded, so there's nothing to fill in. Reports are a licence feature. If you can't see them, ask your platform admin.
