On a saved job, press **Job sheet / handover pack**.

- **Job sheet:** the work done, findings, recommendations, and parts and labour. Prices are optional.
- **Engine handover pack** (when the job has a build sheet): everything above, plus:
  - the build specifications;
  - clearances and torque figures;
  - the next service;
  - your shop's terms.

Press **Print / save as PDF**, then choose a printer or *Save as PDF* in the print window.

Set the terms printed at the bottom in [Shop settings](/help/shop-settings).
