The shared calendars hold every series, track and major-event date that teams pull into their own calendars. As a platform admin you keep them right. Open **Platform admin → Shared calendars**.

Use **‹** and **›** to change the year. Anything you save here goes live for every team straight away.

![Major events on the shared calendars](img/calendar-admin.webp)

## Calendars

Choose a view:

- **Series**: every shared series for the year, including new ones with no dates yet. Tap one to see and manage its rounds. **Edit series** changes its name, season, class or other details.
- **Tracks**: every track with dates this year. Tap one for its dates.
- **Major events**: titles, championships and classics, such as the Australian Sprintcar Title.
- **All dates**: everything for the year.

### Add or change dates

- **Add a date**: fill in the event name, date (and end date for a multi-day meeting), track, series and round, and the classes running. Tick **Major event** for a title or classic. Adding from a series, track or the Major events view fills that in for you.
- **Edit**: change a date. Teams with it on their calendar are told it changed and can update their entry.
- **Delete**: removes the date from the shared calendar. Teams that already added it keep their own entry. To keep it visible but show it's off, edit it and set **Status** to **Cancelled** instead.
- **New series** (in the Series view): adds a series every team can use. Name and season are required.
- **Import a calendar**: read the dates from a link, iCal feed, PDF, photo, screenshot or pasted text, check them, then save. The AI reading counts against the team you're signed in to, so that team needs the AI add-on (iCal doesn't).

## Suggestions

Teams suggest missing dates and corrections. The **Suggestions** tab shows how many are waiting.

![A suggested change with the old and new date](img/calendar-suggestions.webp)

Each suggestion shows the team and person who sent it, when, where the dates came from, and any note. A **change** shows the suggested values beside the current ones, with the differences highlighted.

- **Approve** puts the date live for every team, or applies the change to the shared date.
- **Reject** sends it back. Type the reason first; the team sees it.

When a suggestion uses a series that only that team has, approving it shares the series too. If a shared series with the same name and season already exists, that one is used. A track that's only in that team's list becomes a plain track name on the shared date.

## From a team's calendar

You can also manage shared dates from **Team Management → Calendar → Shared calendars** in any team you belong to. There, **Add a date**, **Edit** and **Import a calendar** publish straight away. Untick **Publish to every team now** to save one as a suggestion instead.
