The shared series and track calendars are used by every team. Teams suggest missing dates and corrections, and you approve them. Open **Platform admin → Calendar suggestions**.

![A suggested change with the old and new date](img/calendar-suggestions.webp)

Each suggestion shows the team and person who sent it, when, where the dates came from, and any note. A **change** shows the suggested values beside the current ones, with the differences highlighted.

## Approve or reject

- **Approve** puts the date live for every team, or applies the change to the shared date. Teams with that date on their calendar see the change and can update their event.
- **Reject** sends it back. Type the reason first; the team sees it.

When a suggestion uses a series that only that team has, approving it shares the series too. If a shared series with the same name and season already exists, that one is used. A track that's only in that team's list becomes a plain track name on the shared date.

## Adding dates yourself

In any team you belong to, open **At the Track → Calendar → Series & track calendars**. As a platform admin, **Add a date**, **Edit** and **Import a calendar** publish straight away. Untick **Publish to every team now** to save it as a suggestion instead.

To add a series for everyone, create it under **Series → New series** and tick **Shared directory — visible to all teams**.
