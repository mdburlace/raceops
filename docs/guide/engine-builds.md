Each engine is an [asset](/help/assets) (category **Engine**). Each time it's built or freshened, it gets a build sheet.

## Start a build sheet

1. Go to **Engine Builds** (or **Engine Shop → Build sheets**).
2. Press **New build** beside the engine (or **New engine** first, if it isn't listed), then pick the build type (fresh build, freshen…).
3. Fill in the sections: block, crank, rods, pistons, cam, heads, valvetrain, measurements, torque specs and dyno results.
4. Press **Save**.

For engine shops, **New build sheet** on a job fills in the engine, customer and work order for you.

## Worked out for you

- **Clearances & checks**, from the measurements you've entered:
  - main, rod and cam bearing oil clearances;
  - piston-to-wall;
  - ring gap per inch of bore;
  - rod ratio, piston speed and bobweight.
- **Calculator buttons** beside fields such as cam centreline, chamber cc, ring gap and dyno HP open the engine builder [calculators](/help/calculators), filled in from the sheet.

## Compare builds

Tick two or more builds and press **Compare builds** to see them side by side, with the differences highlighted.

## Engines built by someone else

If your engine builder uses RaceOps, they can link the engine to your team. You then see their build history here. See [Engines from your engine builder](/help/linked-engines).
