**Time:** about 30 minutes. **You need:** a platform admin account.

## Exercise 1: Create a practice team (5 min)

1. Go to **Platform admin → Teams & licences**.
2. Under **Create a team**, tick **Practice team**. The name fills in as *Practice team* and the team type as race team and engine shop. Change the name if you run several, e.g. *Practice — Hart Racing*.
3. To have the trainer own it, enter their **Owner's email**. Otherwise you're added as its owner.
4. Press **Create team**.

**When it's right:** the team is listed. Open it with **Join & open**: the **Practice team** banner shows, with Car 17, tonight's meeting and job J-1001 ready to use.

## Exercise 2: Invite the trainees (5 min)

In the practice team, go to **Team Management → Members & invites** and invite each trainee **with the role they'll have in the real team** (crew chief, mechanic, driver…). They then see exactly what they'll see for real.

**When it's right:** each trainee has an invite link. Practice teams use seats like any other team, so leave the seats on **Plan default** or set enough.

## Exercise 3: Announce training day (5 min)

1. Go to **Platform admin → Settings**.
2. Add an **announcement** such as *Training Saturday 9am — practice teams available*, with an end date.

**When it's right:** the banner shows to everyone until the end date.

## Exercise 4: Keep the shared calendars current (10 min)

1. Go to **Platform admin → Shared calendars**.
2. Add a date for a series, and open **Requests** to see what teams have sent.

See [Shared calendars (platform admin)](/help/calendar-suggestions).

## Exercise 5: Capture an idea from the day (5 min)

Trainees often spot things worth improving.

1. Go to **Platform admin → Feedback & ideas** and press **New idea**.
2. Link it to the functionality area it's about and save it.

See [Feedback and ideas (platform admin)](/help/feedback-admin).

## When the group's done

Reset the practice team (**Team Management → Members & invites → Practice team → Reset practice data**) for the next group. You can also remove trainees from it.

## Check yourself

<details><summary>Can a practice team's data reach a real team?</summary>
No. It's a separate team. Only the shared directories (tracks, shared calendars) are common to every team, and practice teams don't add to them.
</details>
