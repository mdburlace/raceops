**Business → Partnerships** has three tabs: **Sponsors**, **Paint schemes** and **Hospitality**.

## The sponsor pipeline

Every sponsor, and every prospect, sits in a column: **Prospect → Pitched → Signed → Renewal due** (and **Ended**). Change a sponsor's **Stage** as it moves.

## The sponsor record

Press **New sponsor** (or **Edit** on a sponsor) to record:

- **Sponsor**, **Contact** (their day-to-day contact) and **Billing contact** (who invoices go to). Name, role, email and phone are kept on each contact.
- **Website & social**: their links, one per line. The sponsor page shows them as links.
- **Stage**, **Level** (Naming, Major, Associate, Supporter, In kind, or your own), **Industry**, **Deal value**, the dates and the **Contract**.
- Under **Relationship**: **Open to renewing?** (Yes, Maybe, No, Not asked yet) and when it was last discussed, **Exclusive in their industry** with the **Exclusivity terms**, **Activation notes**, and the **Interaction history**.

Open a sponsor to see the deal and what you promised. **Proposal** prints a proposal with your recent results, cars, crowd and social numbers; **Season report** prints what was delivered. Both save as a PDF.

## What you promised — and the proof

On the sponsor, list each promise under **What we promised**: **Logo on car** (say where), **Posts per event**, **Hospitality passes**, an appearance or anything else. The sponsor page fills in the progress for you:

- **Logo on car:** a paint scheme on the car that lists the sponsor, and photos in the media library tagged with them.
- **Posts per event:** results posts marked **Posted** that tag the sponsor, counted for each meeting in the deal.
- **Hospitality passes:** guests of the sponsor who checked in.

Other promises — **Suit / helmet**, **Pit & trailer signage**, **Website**, **Newsletter**, **Social shout-out**, **PA announcement**, **Podium / award**, **Car display**, an appearance — have a **Status**: **Planned → Designed → Approved by sponsor → Deployed**. Set it on the sponsor with the **Status date**, and pick a **Proof photo** from the media library. **Deployed** counts as done, and a promise with a due date drops off Reminders once it's deployed. The season report and the sponsor's page show where each one is up to.

## Relationship and follow-ups

The sponsor's page has a **Relationship** section:

- **Interaction history**: every meeting, call and email, newest first. Add one at the bottom: the date, **How**, **What happened** and, if you need to get back to them, **Follow up by**, then **Add to history**.
- A follow-up date shows on the sponsor, in the pipeline and in **Reminders** until you add a later entry.
- **Activation notes** and the **Exclusivity terms** are shown here too, and **Open to renewing?** shows at the top.

## Exclusivity

Give each sponsor an **Industry** (e.g. *Tyres*, *Fuel*). Tick **Exclusive in their industry** for a sponsor that must be the only one in it. If another sponsor in the same industry is in play while the deal runs (prospect, pitched, signed or renewal due), both sponsors' pages show **Exclusivity clash**, so you can sort it out before you sign.

## Paint schemes and decals

**Paint schemes → New paint scheme**: the car, the dates it's on, the sponsors on it, their logo positions and a photo. A paint scheme still on the car that carries an ended sponsor is flagged. List the spare **Decals** with a reorder level — low stock shows in Reminders.

## Hospitality

**Hospitality** lists the guests for an event. Add each guest with their sponsor and passes. At the gate or pit, tap **Check in** as they arrive — it works with no signal. Tick **Agreed to be contacted / photographed** where privacy law needs consent (GDPR in the UK and Europe).

## The sponsor's own page

On a sponsor, **Make a sponsor page link** gives a private link (no login) that shows the sponsor their deal (without amounts), what was promised and delivered, results at the meetings in the deal, posts, and photos marked **OK to publish** that show them. **New link** replaces it; **Turn off** stops it working.

To list your signed sponsors on your team's public calendar page, tick **Show on our public page** on each sponsor and **Show our sponsors** under **Calendar → Publish**.

**Who can do this:** roles with Partnerships → edit (the built-in Partnerships Manager). Deal values only show to roles with **See costs, prices and amounts**. Partnerships must be in your team's plan.
