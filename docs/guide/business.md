**Business** in the sidebar holds the modules that keep the team running off the track: [Parts orders](/help/parts-orders), [Finance](/help/finance), [Transport](/help/transport), [Partnerships](/help/partnerships) and [Marketing](/help/marketing). You see the ones your role and your team's plan include.

Everything in them hangs off the **event**. Open an event and, under its sessions, you see its crew, trip plans and load lists, parts orders, expenses and income, its profit and loss, hospitality guests, photos and results posts — each with **+ Add**, which starts the record already linked to the event.

## Reminders

**Business → Reminders** (and **Coming up** on Home) lists everything with a date that's close, or anything running low, from what your role can see:

| Reminder | When it shows |
|---|---|
| Driver licence or medical expiring | 60 days before |
| Vehicle registration or inspection due | 30 days before |
| Bill not yet paid | 7 days before its due date |
| Contract renewal or notice date | 60 days before |
| Sponsor deal ending | 60 days before |
| Sponsor deliverable with a due date | 14 days before |
| Merchandise or decals at or below their reorder level | straight away |
| Budget category at its warning level (80% unless you change it) | straight away — needs “See costs, prices and amounts” |
| Parts order waiting for approval | for people who can approve them |

Reminders are worked out on your device from the records themselves, so they work with no signal. Fix the record (renew the licence, pay the bill, restock) and the reminder goes.

## The crew list and race night

Each event has a **Crew** section: who's going and their job on the night (for example *Tyres*, *Car chief*, *Hauler*).

1. Open the event and press **Edit crew** (or **Edit event** and go to **Crew**).
2. Add a row for each person: pick them, then type their job.
3. Press **Save**.

On race night (from the day before the event to the day after), each person on the crew list sees a **Race night** card on Home with the screens for their job:

- **Driver:** the debrief — Track conditions & car feedback on the latest sheet.
- **Car chief** or **mechanic:** the setup sheet's change list.
- **Tyres:** the tyre sheet (targets against what was set).
- **Crew chief:** the setup sheets and change list.
- **Hauler** or **transport:** the load list and trip plan.
- **Partnerships** or **hospitality:** guest check-in.
- **Marketing** or **media:** a results post and adding photos.

**Who can do this:** anyone who can edit events fills in the crew list. Everyone sees their own race-night card.
