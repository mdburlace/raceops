The built-in **Marketing Manager** role runs [Marketing](/help/marketing) and can edit partnerships.

## After every meeting

1. Open the results post (yours, or the one a crew member drafted), check it and **Approve** it.
2. **Share…** it from your phone, then **Mark as posted** with the link.
3. Add the night's best photos to the media library, tagged with the sponsors shown, and tick **OK to publish**.

## Every month

- Check merchandise stock — low items are in Reminders.
- Keep the team photo fresh: **Team → Team profile → Change team photo** (new livery, new season). See [Team and member photos](/help/team-photos).
