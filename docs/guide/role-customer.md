Engine owners don't need to be part of your team. They get your engine's information in one of two ways.

## Owners without RaceOps: the engine page link

A private web page with no login. It shows the engine, the service interval and last service, every build with specs and clearances, and the work history. Prices show only for jobs where you tick **Show prices to the customer**. See [Share an engine page](/help/share-engine-page).

## Owners who race with RaceOps: a team link

The engine shows in their own RaceOps under **Engine Builds → Engines from your engine builder**, with your full history. They choose whether their laps count on your service tracker. See [Link an engine to the owner's team](/help/link-owner-team), and their side in [Engines from your engine builder](/help/linked-engines).

## A message you can send

> Hi, here's the page for your engine: build specs, clearances and everything we've done to it. It updates as we work on it, and nothing needs installing: [link]
>
> If your team uses RaceOps, use this invite instead, and the engine will appear under Engine Builds: [invite link]. If you tick "Count our laps", we'll see when it's due for a freshen.

What owners never see: your internal notes, parts costs, margins, and any other customer's engines.
