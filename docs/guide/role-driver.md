The built-in **Driver / Viewer** role can see events, setups, tracks, gears and cars, but not change them.

## What you can do

- **Before you go out:** open the session's setup sheet to see the gear, stagger, pressures and changes.
- **Look back:** open earlier events at this track, or use **Setups → Search conditions & feedback**, then **Compare**. [Compare and search setups](/help/compare-search)
- **Ask:** with the AI add-on, *Ask RaceOps* answers questions like "what did we change after I said it was loose off?" [Ask RaceOps](/help/ask-raceops)
- **Calculators:** lap time to speed, lap analysis and more. [Calculators](/help/calculators)

## Getting your feedback in

Your feedback drives the setup advisor, so it matters that it's recorded.

- Tell the crew chief how the car was. They record it in the sheet (or use **Voice debrief** with you).
- If your team wants you to enter it yourself, ask the owner to give your role **Edit** on setup sheets. [Roles and permissions](/help/roles-permissions)
