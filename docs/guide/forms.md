Every sheet in RaceOps is built from a form your team can change: setup sheets, checklists, assets, dyno runs, build sheets and jobs.

1. Go to **Team → Forms** and open a form.
2. Change it:
   - **+ Field**, **+ Section** or **+ Sub-heading** to add;
   - move sections and fields with the ↑ ↓ arrows;
   - open a field to change its label, type, unit or options, or mark it required;
   - ✕ removes a section or field. Data already recorded is kept, just hidden.
3. Press **Save form**. **Discard** throws your changes away.

**Duplicate** copies a form, e.g. to make a midget setup sheet alongside the sprintcar one.

## Field descriptions (ⓘ)

Every standard field has an ⓘ description: what it's for, what to enter and where to get the number. In a field's settings you can write your own description and "where to find it", including for custom fields. Left blank, the standard text is used.

## Copying into the next session

A setup sheet section's settings decide whether its values copy into the next session's sheet. Settings copy; results such as hot pressures, travel and finishes don't.

New standard fields added in RaceOps updates are added to your forms automatically. Your own changes are kept.

**Who can do this:** the owner, or a role with **Edit forms & fields**. Everyone can *see* forms, because sheets are built from them.
