The built-in **Car Chief** role is the Mechanic role plus the setup sheet's **Change list**. Everything a mechanic does is in [Mechanic](/help/role-mechanic).

## The change list

Each setup sheet has a **Change list**: every change the crew chief calls for the session (e.g. *RR rebound −1, was 9, now 8*). The crew chief adds them by hand, or with **Add changes from this sheet**, which lists everything changed against the session the sheet was copied from.

1. On race night, tap **Change list** on your Home card (or open the session's sheet and go to **Change list**).
2. Make each change on the car, then tick **Done** beside it. The tick records who did it and when.
3. Press **Save**.

You can't change the rest of the sheet — only the change list. That gives the team a record of what was actually done to the car.
