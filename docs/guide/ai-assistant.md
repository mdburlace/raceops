The AI assistant is an add-on. It's off until your platform admin turns it on, and your team's plan must include it.

## What it does

- **Fill from photo, PDF or Excel.** Reads paper sheets and files into forms. See [Fill forms from photos, PDFs and spreadsheets](/help/import-files).
- **Voice debrief.** Turns spoken feedback into setup sheet feedback. See [Track conditions, feedback and voice debrief](/help/feedback-debrief).
- **Ask RaceOps.** Questions about your records in plain English. See [Ask RaceOps](/help/ask-raceops).
- **Talk it through** in the setup advisor, and **guide import.** See [Setup advisor](/help/setup-advisor).

Everything is shown for you to check before it changes a record. AI needs a connection; everything else works offline.

## What's sent

Only the photo, file, debrief or question being worked on is sent to the AI service. For Ask RaceOps, the records it looks up are sent too. Text inside your records is treated as data, never as instructions. Each person is limited to 40 AI requests per 10 minutes.

## Turn it on or off for your team

1. Go to **Team → Members & invites**.
2. The **AI assistant** card shows the status and this month's spend against the allowance.
3. Press **Turn AI off for this team** (or **on**).

**Who can do this:** the owner, or a role with **Edit team settings**. To stop just some people using it, untick **AI assistant** in their role under [Roles and permissions](/help/roles-permissions).

## Costs

Each team has a monthly allowance in US$, set by your platform admin. When it's used up, AI stops until next month. See [Platform admin](/help/platform-admin) for models, prices and allowances.
