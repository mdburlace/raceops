Open it from **At the Track → Setup advisor**, or press **Setup advisor** on a saved sheet. From a sheet, the problem is filled in from the driver and crew feedback.

1. Pick what the car is doing, e.g. loose or tight on entry, centre or exit, or no rear bite.
2. Choose track conditions, the car and the track.

## What's worked for you

The advisor finds every sheet where that problem was reported. It looks at what was changed for the next session, and whether the complaint was gone afterwards. For example: *Stagger ↓ tried 4×, gone next session 3×, still there 1×*. Sessions at the same track, with the same car or in the same conditions count for more.

This works offline, and gets better the more feedback you record.

## Guide recommendations

Remedies from the tuning guides for that problem. Each shows:

- its effectiveness rating, where the guide gives one;
- your team's results when you made that change;
- the sheet's current value;
- a **guides disagree** flag where two guides point opposite ways.

**+ Add to changes** adds a remedy to the sheet's *Changes for next session*. With the AI add-on, **Talk it through** weighs your history against the guides and suggests one to four changes in order.

## Tuning guides

**Tuning guides** lists the guides. Two shared guides come with every team; only platform admins edit those. To add your own:

1. Press **New guide**.
2. Type the rules in with **Add rule**, or import a guide from a PDF, photo or Excel file (AI add-on) and check the rules before saving.

Link a rule to a setup setting and direction (e.g. *Stagger ↓*) so it can be matched against your history.
