1. Go to **Team → Performance reviews** and press **New review**.
2. Pick the member, the reviewer, the date and the period covered.
3. Pick the role being reviewed (Crew Chief, Mechanic, Driver…).
4. Rate preparation, setup decisions, communication, teamwork, reliability and safety out of 5. The overall score is the average.
5. Add strengths and other comments, then press **Save**.

Reviews are private to the roles that can view them. The built-in Crew Chief role can't see reviews; give a role access under [Roles and permissions](/help/roles-permissions). Reviews are a licence feature.
