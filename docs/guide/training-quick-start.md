Do this first, with your phone in your hand. By the end you can use RaceOps at the track tonight.

## 1. Sign in (1 minute)

Open the invite link you were sent, or your team's RaceOps address. New to RaceOps? The invite asks for your name and a password (8 characters or more).

## 2. Install it on your phone (2 minutes)

Installed, RaceOps opens full screen like any other app and keeps working with no signal.

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the menu (⋮), then **Install app**.

**Profile & device → Install on this device** shows these steps for your phone.

## 3. Find your way around (3 minutes)

- **Home** shows what needs attention: the next event, services due and recent sheets.
- The sidebar (or the bar along the bottom on a phone) has the areas:
  - **At the Track** — events, setup sheets, the setup advisor, between-round checks, gears and tyres;
  - **In the Workshop** — maintenance, assets, the shock and bar dyno;
  - **Engine Builds** (or **Engine Shop**) — engines, jobs and customers;
  - **Team Management** — the calendar, cars, people and reports.

  You only see what your role and your team's plan include.
- Every page has a **?** beside its title. Tap it for the guide article about that page.
- Every form field has an **ⓘ**. Tap it to see what goes there and where the number comes from.

## 4. Work without signal (2 minutes)

Everything you enter saves on your phone first, then syncs when there's a connection. The badge at the top right shows where things are:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Synced** | Everything's on the server |
| **Syncing** | Sending or fetching now |
| **Waiting to sync** | Saved on this phone. It goes up when you're back in signal |
| **Offline** | No connection. Keep working |

Try it:
1. Turn on flight mode.
2. Change a note on any record and save it. The badge shows **Waiting to sync**.
3. Turn flight mode off and watch it change to **Synced**.

## 5. Get help, and give feedback (1 minute)

- **Help & guide** in the sidebar holds the whole guide, including the guide for your role. It works offline.
- **Feedback & roadmap** is where you report a problem or ask for something new. See [Feedback and the roadmap](/help/feedback).
