You run the car. RaceOps keeps every session's setup, the conditions and the result, so next time you can start from what worked.

## Before the event

1. **Create the event** with the track, car and date. [Events and sessions](/help/events-sessions)
2. **Look back.** Open the last visit to this track, or use **Setups → Search conditions & feedback** for nights like the forecast. [Compare and search setups](/help/compare-search)
3. **Check the parts.** Home and the Workshop hub list shocks, bars and engines near their service limit. [Assets](/help/assets)
4. **Pick a starting point.** The last session, last time out, or a saved baseline.

## At the track: every session

1. **+ Add session** on the event. The new sheet copies the previous session's setup. [Fill in a setup sheet](/help/setup-sheets)
2. **Weather.** Pull it, or type your station's readings and calculate. [Track weather](/help/weather)
3. **Make your changes:** gear, jets, bars, shocks and adjusters, pressures, stagger, wing. Jump with the section bar and use **−/+** for quick tweaks; changes show in blue with what they were. [Quick editing](/help/setup-sheet-layout) Pick parts from the asset lists so their laps count. [Shocks, adjusters and bump rubbers](/help/shocks-on-setup)
4. **Fuel.** Enter planned laps, check the prediction, then **Use as fuel started**. [Plan fuel for a session](/help/fuel-planner)
5. **After the session,** record:
   - laps, start and finish;
   - fuel finished;
   - hot pressures and stagger;
   - travel and tach.
6. **Feedback.** Track conditions, driver and crew-lead feel, and changes for next session. Tap, type or use **Voice debrief**. [Feedback and voice debrief](/help/feedback-debrief)
7. **Stuck?** Press **Setup advisor** on the sheet to see what's fixed this before, and what the guides say. [Setup advisor](/help/setup-advisor)

No signal? Keep going. Everything saves on the phone and syncs later. [Work offline](/help/offline-sync)

## After the night

- **Compare sessions** on the event to see what changed and what it did.
- Save a good setup as a **baseline**.
- Hand the workshop list to the mechanics: [checklists and schedules](/help/maintenance).
