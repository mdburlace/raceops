Every RaceOps update adds a line here, and the articles it links to are updated in the same release. For each release's new features, improvements, bug fixes and notes, see [Release notes](/help/release-notes) (**Help & guide → Release notes**).

## October 2026

- **Ten more training videos.** New narrated videos for working without signal, the race-night change list, the tyre sheet, quick add, a shock rebuild, parts orders, loading the hauler, a sponsor's promises and payments, making a role, and backing up and restoring the team. They're with their exercises and on the [Training videos](/help/training-videos) page.
- **Narrated training videos.** The training videos now have a voice-over that explains each step as it happens, with the caption bar as before, and captions of every word spoken behind the player's **CC** button. Every video is in one place under **Help & guide → Training → Training videos**, as well as at the top of its role's exercises. [Training videos](/help/training-videos)
- **Sponsor payments, in kind, inventory links, trip costs and crew pay.** Sponsors get a payment schedule (outstanding and overdue, **Mark received** records the income, Reminders before each instalment is due) and in-kind contributions recorded at fair value as income and a matching cost. **Add to inventory** makes the part or tyre an expense or parts order bought, and an item's page shows everything **Spent on this**. A trip's fuel and tolls go into Finance with **Add to Finance**, and **Add crew costs to Finance** on the event page drafts per diems and pay from each person's contract. [Partnerships](/help/partnerships#payments-and-in-kind), [Finance](/help/finance#crew-pay-and-per-diems)
- **Full team budget and a fuller sponsor record.** The budget is now in groups — vehicle & hardware, parts & consumables, personnel, logistics & travel, event & admin, marketing, contingency — with new categories (car purchase & lease, per diems & catering, track hire & testing, crash & failure repairs), a **contingency reserve** and an **income budget**. Income adds seat fees, series stipends and in-kind sponsorship. Sponsors get a billing contact, website & social links, industry and exclusivity (with a clash warning), **Open to renewing?**, activation notes, an **interaction history** with follow-ups in Reminders, and more kinds of promise, each with a status and proof photo. [Finance](/help/finance#budget), [Partnerships](/help/partnerships#the-sponsor-record)
- **Team and member photos, and more quick add.** Add a team photo under **Team → Team profile** (the owner, or a role given **Change the team photo**, such as the Marketing Manager); it shows beside the team's name in the menu. Each person can add their photo on **Profile & device**, and team managers can add one for any member. On the setup sheet, **Turns** has − and + by half a turn, and the quick add screen shows **Last session**'s value and lets you **Say it**. [Team and member photos](/help/team-photos), [Quick add screen](/help/setup-sheet-layout#quick-add-screen)
- **Quick add screen, angle finder and more on the setup sheet.** Long-press a number box on a setup sheet for a big keypad with the team's common values, and sixteenths for block height, turns, ride height and stagger. Angles have **Pick** and an **Angle finder** that reads your phone's tilt sensor laid on the wing. **Blocked** is now **Block height**, with **Turns** for the torsion stop adjusters; **Chassis** adds rake, tilt, the RR radius rod, birdcage angles and the Jacobs ladder; and **Race** records low, high and highest rpm. [Quick add screen](/help/setup-sheet-layout#quick-add-screen), [Fill in a setup sheet](/help/setup-sheets#fill-it-in)
- **Events in more than one series, round prefixes and locked setup sheets.** An event or shared date can count towards several series at once, each with its own round (**Also counts towards**). Add events to a series from its page, and platform admins can add shared dates from **Edit series** on a shared series. Rounds now have a prefix — **Round**, **Night** or **Prelude**, or your own — shown in lists and calendars instead of *Rd*. **Lock sheet** stops a finished setup sheet being changed; the owner, and roles given **Unlock locked setup sheets** (such as the crew chief), can unlock it. [More than one series](/help/events-sessions#more-than-one-series), [Lock a finished sheet](/help/setup-sheets#lock-a-finished-sheet)
- **What happened each day of a multi-day event.** Each day's tab on a multi-day event records what happened that day — raced the feature, missed it, withdrew (and why) or didn't race — with the result in a few words and notes. The meeting's overall result is worked out from the days until you pick one, and the days' results show in lists and, with results on, on your published calendar. [Multi-day events](/help/events-sessions#multi-day-events)
- **Team roles and the Business area.** New built-in roles — Driver (fills in the debrief), Car Chief (ticks off the change list), Tire Specialist (tyre sheet and work log), Viewer, Transport, Business Manager, Partnerships Manager and Marketing Manager — and **Add standard roles** for existing teams. A person can hold more than one role. Setup sheets get a **Change list** and **Tyre targets**; events get a **Crew** list that puts each person's race-night jobs on Home. The new **Business** area has [Parts orders](/help/parts-orders) with approval, [Finance](/help/finance) with a profit and loss for every event and an accounting export for every region, [Transport](/help/transport), [Partnerships](/help/partnerships) and [Marketing](/help/marketing), plus [Reminders](/help/business) and [Region & currency](/help/region-currency). [Business, reminders and race night](/help/business)
- **A line for each day in lists.** A multi-day event in **Events**, **Calendar**, the shared calendars, series and track pages, **At the Track** and the printed calendar now has a line for each day under it: the date, the day's name (or *Day 1 of 2*), its start time and whether it was rained out, joined to the event by a line down the left. On the Calendar, tap a day to open it. [Multi-day events](/help/events-sessions#multi-day-events)
- **Release notes.** **Help & guide → Release notes** lists every release, newest first, with its summary and date, new features, improvements, bug fixes and notes. The bottom of the sidebar shows your version, and Home tells you once when the app has been updated. Platform admins can link each release to its code on GitHub. [Release notes](/help/release-notes)
- **Multi-day events.** Tick **Runs over more than one day** on an event or shared calendar date, then set each day: its date, a name such as *Night 1* or *Finals*, a start time, and whether it was rained out. Days can have gaps. It stays one event: the event page has a tab per day, setup sheets pick their day, and the calendar, month view and iCal feeds show each day. [Multi-day events](/help/events-sessions#multi-day-events)
- **Engine tunes and engine checks.** **Engine tunes** keeps each engine's tune in versions, with one active: pill settings by ADR or density altitude, the ending tune and the fuel system. Setup sheets show the pills for tonight's weather and **Use this jetting** fills them in. Engine builders can share a tune with the owner's team. **In the Workshop → Engine checks** records the between-race engine service, and flags weak springs, rising leak-down, lash off the tune and plug readings. [Engine tune sheets](/help/engine-tunes), [Workshop engine checks](/help/engine-checks)
- **Workshop setup sheets.** **In the Workshop → Workshop setups** records how each car is set up on the shop floor: reference conditions, front and rear suspension and geometry, tyres and wheels, wings, drivetrain and weights. Link one to an event as its baseline, and the track sheets start from it. [Workshop setup sheets](/help/workshop-setups)
- **Recently deleted.** Deleted something by mistake? **Recently deleted** (in the sidebar) puts it back. Everyone can recover what they made or deleted; team managers can recover anything in the team; platform admins anything at all. Deleted items are kept for 90 days unless your platform admin sets otherwise. [Recently deleted](/help/recently-deleted)
- **Export your team's data.** **Team Management → Backups & export** exports the team's records as CSV spreadsheets, XML or JSON, with or without attached files, to download or to one of your destinations. [Team backups and export](/help/backups-team)
- **Backups and restore.** Platform admins can back up the whole server, now or on a schedule, to a server folder, S3-compatible storage, Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, and restore everything, bring one team back as it was, or move RaceOps to a new server. Each team can back up its own data, set its own schedule and destination, and put the team back as it was. [Backups and restore](/help/backups-platform), [Team backups](/help/backups-team)
- **Attach files to feedback and ideas.** Problem reports, requests and platform admins' ideas now take up to 5 files of up to 20 MB each: screenshots, screen recordings, PDFs, Office files, text, CSV, log and ZIP files. Add them when you send, or later from **My submissions**. [Feedback & roadmap](/help/feedback), [Feedback and ideas (platform admin)](/help/feedback-admin)
- **Training kept up to date with every release.** The training pack is now checked against every update, and the trainer's guide says what to do when one lands. The quick start and cards now cover **Can’t sync** and **Repair this device**. [Trainer's guide and rollout plan](/help/training-trainers), [Quick reference cards](/help/training-cards)
- **Training pack.** A new **Training** section in Help & guide: a quick start, hands-on exercises for each role with checks of understanding, printable quick reference cards, short videos, and a trainer's guide with a rollout plan. Platform admins can create a **practice team** loaded with demo data, and its owner can reset it between groups. [Training — start here](/help/training)
- **Repair this device.** If one device stays **Offline** while the app works everywhere else, **Profile & device → Repair this device** clears what that browser has stored for RaceOps and downloads your data again, keeping your sign-in and any changes waiting to sync. The badge now says **Can’t sync** and points you there when it spots the problem, and the app no longer lets the browser cache its connection to the server. [Sign in, install, and work offline](/help/offline-sync)
- **Save at the top of event forms.** The quick edit on **Our calendar** and the event form (from **Events** or **All details**) now have **Save** and **Cancel** at the top. [Calendar](/help/calendar), [Events and sessions](/help/events-sessions)
- **Round and track on every event list, and buttons at the top of the date form.** Events on **Events**, **At the Track**, **Calendar**, series and track pages now show the round and track first, e.g. *Rd 3 · Valley Speedway*. When you edit or suggest a calendar date, **Save** (or **Send for approval**) and **Cancel** are at the top of the form. [Events and sessions](/help/events-sessions), [Calendar](/help/calendar)
- **Bars, tyres, wheels and wings from inventory on the setup sheet.** Each corner's **Bars**, **Tires** and **Wheels** list the items meant for that corner and car first, or choose **Enter manually…** to type what's on the car and **+ Save to inventory** to keep it. The **Wing** section now has a **Top wing** and a **Front wing**, each picked from your wings, and the front wing has its own **wicker**. Wings in Assets can be set to the **Top** or **Front** position. [Shocks, bars, tyres, wheels and wings on the setup sheet](/help/shocks-on-setup)
- **Feedback and the roadmap.** Report a problem or ask for an improvement or new feature from **Feedback & roadmap** in the sidebar, follow what happens under **My submissions**, and vote on the **Roadmap**. Platform admins capture their own ideas, review everything into **Now**, **Next** or **Later**, and send work to Claude as a GitHub issue or a ready-made brief. [Feedback and the roadmap](/help/feedback), [Feedback and ideas](/help/feedback-admin)
- **Fixed: missing records after an access change.** When your role or your team's licence changed, the app could show **Synced** with some records missing until you pressed **Re-download all data**. It now always downloads everything again. [Install and work offline](/help/offline-sync)
- **Choose rounds for a partial series.** A partial series entry lists every round in the series, with **Tick all to come** and **Clear**. **Choose rounds** opens under the entry, and unticking a round marks it **Not going**. [Calendar](/help/calendar)
- **More for the shared calendars.** **Follow** a series or track and **Calendar → Updates** tells you when its dates are added, moved, cancelled or get results. The new **Clashes** view shows meetings on the same day in the same region. Shared dates can carry **results** and the winner, reported by teams. Every shared series and track has a **public page** and a **Subscribe** link. Admins can **ask a team a question** about a request, appoint a series' or track's own team as an **official** (with optional auto-approve), add **official iCal feeds** that are checked automatically, **merge** duplicate series and tracks, and see **history** with restore. If the server has email set up, admins and teams get **emails**, which you can turn off under **Profile & device**. [Calendar](/help/calendar), [Shared calendars](/help/calendar-suggestions)
- **Share your series, tracks and major events.** Teams can send their own series, tracks, dates and major events to the shared calendars, and request changes to shared ones. Every request is checked for duplicates and clashes, and a platform admin approves or refuses each date. Approved records become the single shared copy: the team's events link to them, and later changes flow to every team. **Calendar → Requests** shows each request's outcome, and each team can keep **Our notes** on shared series, tracks and dates. [Calendar](/help/calendar), [Shared calendars](/help/calendar-suggestions)
- **Initial measurement for shocks and bars.** Mark the dyno run or bar test taken at purchase or first build. Later tests show how far they've moved from it, and Shock fade measures from it. [Shock and bar dyno](/help/dyno)
- **Calendar quick edit, sanctioning bodies and major event names.** **Edit** on a calendar entry opens a quick edit under it, where you can set the series and round, the sanctioning body and a major event's name. Series, events and shared dates record the **sanctioning body**. Editing a shared date opens under that date too. [Calendar](/help/calendar)
- **Calendar views and major events.** The Calendar has moved to **Team Management** (drivers and crew can still open it). **Shared calendars** now has views for **Series**, **Tracks** and **Major events** such as the Australian Sprintcar Title. Platform admins manage every shared date and series from **Platform admin → Shared calendars**. [Calendar](/help/calendar), [Shared calendars](/help/calendar-suggestions)
- **Reading long files.** Fill from photo, PDF or Excel now copes with long scans, such as a 10-page shock dyno printout. They read in the background, so a slow read is no longer cut off, and a shock run only fills the table that matches its adjuster type. If something goes wrong, the message now says why. [Fill forms from photos, PDFs and spreadsheets](/help/import-files)
- **Calendars.** Plan each racing year under **Team Management → Calendar**. Pull dates from the shared series and track calendars, with the right car picked when more than one of your classes is running. Enter a full or partial series, mark each meeting planned or tentative, and record whether you raced the feature or withdrew, and why. Print the year, or publish it as a public page with a subscribe link. Teams can suggest or import dates from a link, PDF, screenshot or iCal, and platform admins approve them. [Calendar](/help/calendar)
- **Torsion bar tests.** Each test now records the bar's serial, maker, model, size, type and corner, its rate at a standard arm travel (3 in on a 15 in arm), compression and rebound, and the split. **Rate baselines** average the bars you choose for each size and type, and every test shows how it compares. [Shock and bar dyno](/help/dyno)
- **Shock dyno test speeds.** A run can be tested at one or more speeds (3.00, 6.00 and 10.00 in/s to start with, or your own), with readings and a curve for each. [Shock and bar dyno](/help/dyno)
- **Fuel in US gallons or litres.** Each sheet records fuel in either unit, **Show in** converts what's on screen, and the team sets the default. [Plan fuel for a session](/help/fuel-planner)
- **Analytics.** New page with Performance (results over time, by track, conditions and the air, handling comments), Setups (what worked, setup drift, your own jet chart) and Engines & parts (service forecast, engine health, shock fade), plus a **Form & what's due** card on Home. [Analytics](/help/analytics)
- **Our tracks starts empty.** Tracks are added when you race at them or link them from the Directory. Classes we race now marks matching tracks in the Directory, with a **Runs our classes** filter, instead of adding them all. [Tracks and classes](/help/tracks)
- **One class list.** A car's **Class** now picks from your team's Classes we race and the shared Race classes list, so cars, tracks and Classes we race use the same names. You can still type a class that isn't listed. [Add your cars](/help/cars)
- **Setup sheet quick editing.** A section bar to jump around, your own section order, folding sections, changes highlighted with what they were, **Changes only**, a **Before / After** switch, − and + buttons, and four-corner values on one row. Shocks are a card per corner, and Wing has its own section. [Quick editing on the setup sheet](/help/setup-sheet-layout)
- **Help & guide.** This guide is now built into the app and works offline. It has guides for each role, a **?** on each page that opens the article for that page, a **Set up your team** checklist on Home, and **Print / PDF** for the whole guide. [Using this guide](/help/help-guide)
- **Shocks on setup sheets.** Each corner lists that corner's shocks first. Adjuster boxes match each shock's type, and you can record bump rubbers. [Shocks, adjusters and bump rubbers](/help/shocks-on-setup)
- **Fuel planner.** Predicts the fuel to start with from planned laps and past fuel use, plus the car's new **Minimum fuel (pickup)**. [Plan fuel for a session](/help/fuel-planner)
- **Excel and CSV.** **Fill from photo, PDF or Excel** and table **Paste / import** read spreadsheets. [Fill forms from photos, PDFs and spreadsheets](/help/import-files)
- **Engine shops.** New team type with customers, jobs with parts and labour, and handover packs. You can share an engine page with its owner, or link it to the owner's RaceOps team. [Choose your team type](/help/team-type), [Jobs — parts and labour](/help/shop-jobs)
- **Linked engines.** Choose, per engine, whether your laps count on your engine builder's service tracker. [Engines from your engine builder](/help/linked-engines)
- **Calculators.** 47 calculators for tuning, chassis, gearing, the engine and the workshop. Many open from the field they fill. [Calculators](/help/calculators)
- **Track weather.** Pull the weather for the session, or calculate ADR, density altitude and grains from your own readings. [Track weather](/help/weather)
- **Themes and sidebar.** Pick a theme or make your own. Sidebar sections fold away. [Themes and sidebar](/help/appearance)
- **Setup advisor.** Suggests changes from what's worked for your team before and from tuning guides. [Setup advisor](/help/setup-advisor)
