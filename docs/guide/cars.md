Every event, setup sheet and maintenance checklist belongs to a car.

## Add a car

1. Go to **Team → Cars** and press **New car**.
2. Fill in the number, class, driver and chassis details.
3. Set the three fields the sheets use:
   - **Gear list.** Which quick-change gear chart this car uses, so its setup sheets offer the right gears.
   - **Ring & pinion ratio**, e.g. 4.86. Setup sheets need this to work out the **final drive**.
   - **Minimum fuel (pickup).** The least fuel that keeps the pickup covered under load. The [fuel planner](/help/fuel-planner) adds it to every prediction and warns when a session finishes below it.
4. Press **Save**.

## Class

**Class** offers one list of classes:

1. your team's **Classes we race** (from the Tracks page), shown first;
2. then the standard class list every team shares (e.g. *410 Sprintcars*), kept by your platform admin under **Pick-lists → Race classes**.

If your class isn't listed, type it in. It's best to use the same name your tracks use (e.g. *410 Sprintcars*, not *410 Winged Sprint*). That way, adding it to **Classes we race** links the tracks that run it. See [Tracks and classes](/help/tracks).

Cars added before this change may show an older name such as *410 Winged Sprint*. Open the car and pick the matching class from the list.

**Tip:** to find your minimum fuel, drain the tank and add fuel until the pump picks up cleanly in a turn. Then add a margin.

Untick **Active** for cars you no longer race. They're hidden from pick lists, and their history stays.
