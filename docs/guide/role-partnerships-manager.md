The built-in **Partnerships Manager** role runs [Partnerships](/help/partnerships), can edit marketing and contacts, and sees deal values.

## Common jobs

| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Pitch a new sponsor | **Partnerships → Sponsors → New sponsor**, then **Proposal** |
| See what we still owe a sponsor | The sponsor's **What we promised** |
| Send a sponsor their season report | The sponsor's **Season report**, or their sponsor page link |
| Check guests in at the gate | **Partnerships → Hospitality** |
| See which car still carries an old sponsor | **Partnerships → Paint schemes** |
