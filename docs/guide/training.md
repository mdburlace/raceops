RaceOps training is hands-on. You learn by doing real jobs on a **practice team** full of demo data, so nothing you try can upset your real team.

## The training pack

| Part | What it's for | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [Quick start](/help/training-quick-start) | Sign in, install on your phone, find your way around, work offline | 10–15 min |
| Exercises for your role | Real jobs in the practice team, with what you should see when it's right | 30–60 min |
| [Quick reference cards](/help/training-cards) | One-page cards to print and keep in the trailer or workshop | — |
| [Training videos](/help/training-videos) | Watch a common job being done, with a voice-over explaining each step and captions | 1–3 min each |
| [Trainer's guide](/help/training-trainers) | For whoever runs the training: rollout plan, session plans and sign-off | — |

**Exercises by role:**

- [Crew chief](/help/training-crew-chief)
- [Mechanic](/help/training-mechanic)
- [Driver](/help/training-driver)
- [Engine builder](/help/training-engine-builder)

Start with the quick start, then do the exercises for your role. Each set ends with a few questions to check yourself and a sign-off task.

## The practice team

A practice team is an ordinary RaceOps team loaded with demo data:

- **Cars:** Car 17 (410 sprint) and Car 71 (360 sprint).
- **Parts on Car 17:** the engine *410 #3* (with its tune from Hart Engines in two versions, *2026 v2* active, and two engine checks), shocks, torsion bars, wheels, top and front wings, and tyres. The *RR Penske 8760* shock is nearly due for a rebuild.
- **Racing:** the *Club Championship* series, with eight rounds already raced (two setup sheets each), tonight's meeting *Valley Speedway — Club night* with **Heat 1** done, and round 9 next week.
- **Workshop:** a workshop setup for Car 17 (*Lakeside baseline*, linked to round 9), maintenance schedules (one due), a recent service and a between-round check with an issue noted.
- **Engine shop:** a customer, *Sam Hart*, his engine *Hart 410*, and job *J-1001* in progress.
- **Business:** a parts order waiting for approval (*RR shock rebuild kit*), a season budget with tyres at 90%, an expense, entry and prize money for every round, an unpaid engine bill, the sponsor *Valley Tyres* (signed, with a contract, paint scheme *2026 main*, a photo and a post) and the prospect *Lakeside Motors*, a guest for tonight (*Chris Lee*), the *Hauler* with registration due, a driver licence, the load list template *Sprint car race weekend*, a trip to round 9 and the *Team T-shirt* running low.

While you're in the practice team, a **Practice team** banner shows at the top of every screen. If your login belongs to more than one team, switch with the team list at the top of the sidebar.

A platform admin creates practice teams (see [Platform admin exercises](/help/training-platform-admin)). The practice team's owner can put the demo data back at any time: **Team Management → Members & invites → Practice team → Reset practice data**. Resetting removes everything trainees added or changed. Changes to forms and roles are kept.

## Who can do what

| Job | Who |
|---|---|
| Use the practice team | Anyone invited to it, with the role they're invited as |
| Reset the practice data | The practice team's owner, or anyone with **Edit team settings** |
| Create a practice team | Platform admins |
