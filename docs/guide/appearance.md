Your look is personal. It's saved to your account, so it follows you to every device, and nobody else is affected.

## Themes

Go to **Profile & device → Appearance** and pick one:

{{themes}}

**Sunlight** has extra contrast for the pits in bright sun.

## Make your own

1. In Appearance, press **Create a custom theme**.
2. Pick a light or dark base and five colours: page background, cards, text, accent and sidebar. Borders and soft highlights are worked out from those.
3. The app previews your changes as you go, and warns you if text would be hard to read. Press **Save theme**.

You can keep up to 12 custom themes.

## Sidebar sections

The arrow beside each sidebar heading (At the Track, In the Workshop…) collapses it to just the heading. A dot on a collapsed heading shows that the page you're on is inside it.

Appearance can also show or hide whole sections, and **Collapse all** or **Expand all**.
