**Time:** about 50 minutes. **You need:** the practice team, signed in as a crew chief. A phone is best, as at the track.

**The story:** it's club night at Valley Speedway. Car 17 has run **Heat 1** and the driver says it was loose off the corners. You set the car up for the **Feature**.

![Video: Start the Feature sheet from Heat 1 and make changes](video/training-feature-sheet.webm)

## Exercise 1: Read tonight's heat (5 min)

1. Go to **At the Track → Events** and open **Valley Speedway — Club night**.
2. Open the **Heat 1** sheet for Car 17.
3. Find the driver's feedback in **Track conditions & car feedback**, and the start and finish in **Race**.

**When it's right:** you've found the feedback (*Loose exit*, and the comment *Loose off turn 2*), that the car started 6th and finished 4th, and that the track was *Tacky*.

## Exercise 2: Start the Feature sheet from the heat (10 min)

1. Back on the event, pick **Feature** in the session list beside **+ Add session**, then press **+ Add session**.
2. Under **Copy a session**, **Heat 1** is already chosen (the session before this one). Press **Copy this session**.
3. Make three changes for a loose exit:
   - **Shocks → RR:** take one click of rebound out (the **−** beside *Reb*, from 9 to 8);
   - **Tires → Pressure cold:** RR from 16 to 16.5;
   - **Wing → Top wing angle:** from 13 to 14 (press **+** twice: it moves in half degrees).
4. Press **Save**.

**When it's right:** the sheet shows three changes, each with *was …* under it (*was 9*, *was 16*, *was 13*). Hot pressures, the race result and the feedback were not copied.

## Exercise 3: See only what changed (5 min)

1. On the Feature sheet, tick **Changes only**.
2. Switch **Before / After**: **Before** shows what you set before the session, **After** what you record after it.

**When it's right:** with **Changes only**, just the three fields you changed are shown. See [Quick editing on the setup sheet](/help/setup-sheet-layout).

## Exercise 4: Ask the setup advisor (5 min)

1. On the Heat 1 sheet, press **Setup advisor**.
2. Look at **What's worked for you** for *Loose exit*, and the guide recommendations below it.

**When it's right:** you can name one change the team made before that helped with a loose exit, from the earlier rounds.

## Exercise 5: Compare the heat and the feature (5 min)

1. On the event, press **Compare sessions**.
2. Tick **Changes only**.

**When it's right:** Heat 1 and the Feature sit side by side, with your three changes highlighted.

## Exercise 6: Find what you ran when it was slick (5 min)

1. Go to **Setup sheets → Search conditions & feedback**.
2. Pick **Slick** under track conditions.

**When it's right:** you see the earlier rounds run on a slick track, with stagger and wing angle for each.

## Exercise 7: Save a baseline (5 min)

1. Open your Feature sheet.
2. Tick **Save as a baseline setup** and name it *Valley tacky*. Press **Save**.
3. Go to **Setup sheets → Baselines**.

**When it's right:** *Valley tacky* is listed. Next time, you can start a sheet from it.

## Exercise 8: Start next week from the workshop setup (5 min)

1. Open next week's event, *Lakeside Raceway — Club Championship* (round 9). It shows **Workshop setup: Lakeside baseline**.
2. Press **Start from workshop setup**.
3. Look at the **Chassis** and **Shocks** sections.

**When it's right:** the new sheet says it started from workshop setup *Lakeside baseline*, and the bars, shocks, ride heights, pressures and wing angle are filled in from it. See [Workshop setup sheets](/help/workshop-setups).

## Exercise 9: Pick the pills for tonight's weather (5 min)

1. Open tonight's **Heat 1** sheet and go to the **Engine** section. It shows the motor's tune, *2026 v2*, marked **active**.
2. In **Weather**, type an **ADR** of *91*.
3. Read the panel: the 90–93 row is marked and it says *main pill 70, high-speed 108 psi / 72*.
4. Press **Use this jetting**, then **Save**.

**When it's right:** **Main** shows *70* and **HS** shows *108 psi / 72*, and **Engine tune** shows *2026 v2*. See [Engine tune sheets](/help/engine-tunes).

## Exercise 10: A two-night meeting (5 min)

1. Open **Events** and find *Valley Spring Classic*. It shows **2 days**, with a line under it for each day: *Practice & prelims* and *Finals*, with a night off between them.
2. Open it. There's a tab for each day: **Practice & prelims** and **Finals**, each with its date.
3. Press **Finals**, then **+ Add session** and add a **Feature** sheet. Save it.
4. Open the sheet again and look at **Day**: it lists both days by name and date.
5. Press **Edit event**. Set **Practice & prelims** to **Rained out** and save.

**When it's right:** the Feature sheet is on the **Finals** tab only. **Practice & prelims** is crossed out, and the **Calendar → Month** view marks just the two race days, not the day between. See [Multi-day events](/help/events-sessions#multi-day-events).

## Check yourself

<details><summary>A two-day meeting gets a practice day added before it. Do you make a new event?</summary>
No. Edit the event and press <b>+ Add a day</b> (or set <b>Number of days</b>), then set the new day's date. One event keeps all the days and their sheets together.
</details>

<details><summary>Tonight's ADR is off the end of the engine's chart. What do you do?</summary>
RaceOps offers the nearest row and says the reading is outside the chart. Check with your engine builder before you run it.
</details>

<details><summary>Which values are <b>not</b> copied to a new sheet?</summary>
Hot pressures and hot stagger, shock travel, the race result, conditions and feedback, notes and photos — they belong to the session that ran.
</details>
<details><summary>Why pick the shocks and bars from the list instead of typing them?</summary>
Picked parts collect the session's laps and race meetings, so their service life and history are kept up to date.
</details>
<details><summary>You deleted the wrong setup sheet. Can you get it back?</summary>
Yes. Open <b>Recently deleted</b> in the sidebar and press <b>Recover</b> on it. You can recover what you made or deleted yourself; for anyone else's, ask your team manager.
</details>
<details><summary>You have no signal at the track. Can you still save a sheet?</summary>
Yes. It saves on your phone and syncs when you're back in signal. The badge shows <b>Waiting to sync</b> until then.
</details>

## Sign-off

Run a whole night in the practice team: start a **Heat 2** sheet from Heat 1, make one change, record the result and the driver's feedback after the session, then compare it with Heat 1. A trainer checks it with you.
