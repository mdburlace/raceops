Between sessions, run through the car with a checklist so nothing's missed when time is short.

1. Go to **At the Track → Between-round checks** and press **New check**.
2. Pick the event, car and the session just run (and the day, for multi-day events).
3. Tick each item as it's checked: pressures, lug nuts, tyre wear, stagger, fuel, oil, water, leaks, shocks and the rest. Add a note against anything that needs attention.
4. Press **Save**.

The At the Track hub shows the most recent checks. Bigger jobs belong on the [maintenance checklists](/help/maintenance).

At-track checks are a licence feature. If you can't see them, ask your platform admin.
