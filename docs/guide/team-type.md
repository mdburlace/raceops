The team type decides which areas show in the menu and on Home.

{{team-types}}

- **Race team.** At the Track, In the Workshop, Engine Builds and Team Management.
- **Engine shop.** No At the Track, Cars or Car maintenance. Engine Builds becomes **Engine Shop**, with Jobs, Customers, Customer engines, Build sheets and Calculators. Home shows **In the shop**: jobs by status, jobs due or overdue, and jobs ready for pickup.
- **Both.** Everything, for a team that races its own car and also builds for customers.

## Change it

1. Go to **Team → Members & invites**.
2. In **Team type & engine shop**, pick the type.
3. Press **Save**.

**Who can change it:** the owner, or a role with **Edit team settings**. A platform admin can also set it when creating the team.

Changing the type never deletes anything. It only changes what's shown.
