**Time:** about 25 minutes. **You need:** the practice team on your phone, signed in as a driver. The **Driver / Viewer** role can read everything at the track but doesn't change it.

![Video: Read tonight's setup and see what changed](video/training-driver.webm)

## Exercise 1: Read tonight's setup (5 min)

1. Go to **At the Track → Events** and open **Valley Speedway — Club night**.
2. Open the **Heat 1** sheet. Use the row of section buttons under the title to jump to **Tires**, **Wing** and **Shocks**.

**When it's right:** you can say tonight's cold pressures, the top wing angle (13°) and the stagger (5.5 in).

## Exercise 2: Compare tonight with last time (5 min)

1. On the event, press **Compare sessions**, or go to **Compare** and tick two sheets.
2. Tick **Changes only**.

**When it's right:** you see only what's different between the sheets, side by side.

## Exercise 3: Where are we racing? (5 min)

1. Go to **Team Management → Calendar**.
2. Find the next meeting on **Our calendar**.

**When it's right:** you've found *Rd 9 · Lakeside Raceway* next week.

## Exercise 4: How have I been going? (5 min)

1. Go to **Analytics → Performance**.
2. Look at results over time and your best tracks.

**When it's right:** you can name the track where the car has finished best this season.

## Exercise 5: Tell the team (5 min)

1. Open **Feedback & roadmap** in the sidebar.
2. Look at the **Roadmap**, and press **Vote** on something you'd use.

**When it's right:** your vote shows on the item. If your team lets drivers record feedback on setup sheets, also see [Feedback and debrief](/help/feedback-debrief).

Before a session, open the sheet on your phone and tell the crew chief the cold pressures and the wing angle set for you.
