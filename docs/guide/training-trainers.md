This guide is for whoever introduces RaceOps to a team: usually the owner or crew chief, with the platform admin setting things up.

## Before you start

1. **A practice team.** Ask your platform admin to create one (or create it yourself, see [Training: platform admin](/help/training-platform-admin)). It comes loaded with demo data, and you can reset it between groups.
2. **Invite each trainee with their real role** — crew chief, mechanic, driver — so they see what they'll see for real.
3. **Phones.** Have everyone install RaceOps on their phone first. Most of the real work happens at the track.
4. **Print the cards.** Print the [quick reference cards](/help/training-cards), one set per person and one for the trailer.
5. **Check the videos play** on the room's screen. They need an internet connection.

## Rollout plan for a real team

Introduce RaceOps one area at a time. Start with what pays off quickest, at a real race meeting, before adding the next area.

| Week | Focus | Who | What "done" looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Set up the team | Owner | The **Set up your team** checklist on Home is ticked off: team type, cars, members and roles |
| 1 | Parts | Mechanics | Shocks, bars, wheels, wings and tyres in **Assets** and **Tires**, with corners and cars set |
| 2 | One race meeting on setup sheets | Crew chief, driver | Every session at one meeting has a sheet, copied from the one before, with feedback recorded |
| 3 | Between rounds and the workshop | Mechanics | Between-round checks at the meeting; schedules for engine, shocks and bearings; a weekly checklist done |
| 4 | Plan and review | Owner, crew chief | The racing year on **Calendar**; Analytics and Reports reviewed after the meeting |
| — | Engine shop (if you build engines) | Engine builder | Customers, their engines and every open job in RaceOps; one handover pack printed |

Keep paper sheets going in week 2 as a backup if the crew prefers. Most teams drop them by the second meeting.

## Session plans

Run sessions in the practice team, with phones, not on a projector alone.

**Session 1 — Everyone: quick start (20 minutes)**

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 5 min | Why the team's using RaceOps: one place for setups, parts and service |
| 10 min | Everyone does the [Quick start](/help/training-quick-start) on their own phone, including the flight-mode test |
| 5 min | Questions. Point out **?**, **ⓘ** and **Help & guide** |

**Session 2 — By role (45 minutes each)**

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 5 min | Play the role's video |
| 30 min | The role's exercises, at their own pace ([crew chief](/help/training-crew-chief), [mechanic](/help/training-mechanic), [driver](/help/training-driver), [engine builder](/help/training-engine-builder), [owner](/help/training-owner)) |
| 10 min | **Check yourself** questions together, and anything that tripped people up |

**Session 3 — Race night rehearsal (60 minutes, whole crew)**

Run a pretend meeting in the practice team, each person in their own role:

1. The crew chief starts the **Heat 2** sheet from Heat 1.
2. The mechanic does the between-round check, and notes a problem.
3. The driver reads the sheet and reports how the car was. The crew chief records it.
4. The crew chief starts the **Feature** sheet and makes changes.
5. Everyone turns on flight mode for part of it, to see it keeps working.
6. Afterwards: **Compare sessions**, the setup advisor, and what's due in the workshop.

Then reset the practice team for the next group.

## Sign-off checklist

Someone's ready when they can do their sign-off task without help.

| Role | Sign-off task |
|---|---|
| Crew chief | A whole night: sheets copied session to session, changes made, results and feedback recorded, sessions compared |
| Mechanic | A due job done and recorded (schedule, asset or checklist), and a between-round check |
| Driver | Reads the sheet on their phone and states the cold pressures and wing angle |
| Engine builder | A job from booked in to ready for pickup, with a build sheet and a printed handover pack |
| Owner | Every real crew member invited with the right role, and confirmed they can see what they need |
| Platform admin | A practice team created, trainees invited, and reset after training |

## Keeping the training current

RaceOps is updated regularly, and the training pack is updated in the same release: the exercises, cards, quick start and videos always match the app you're using. When an update lands:

1. Read the [release notes](/help/release-notes) for the update (**Help & guide → Release notes**), then **What's new**. Each What's new line links to the article that explains it.
2. Reset the practice team (**Team Management → Members & invites → Practice team → Reset practice data**), so it has the latest demo data.
3. Run through the exercises for the roles the update touches, before you train anyone on them.
4. Reprint the [quick reference cards](/help/training-cards) if they changed, and swap the old ones in the trailer and workshop.
5. Tell trained crew what's new in their role. A five-minute run-through at the next race meeting is usually enough.

If an exercise doesn't match what you see, send it through **Feedback & roadmap**.

## Tips

- **Reset between groups**, so everyone starts from the same place. Forms and roles you change aren't reset.
- **Use the same names as the exercises** (Car 17, J-1001) when you demonstrate, so trainees can follow along.
- **Collect feedback.** Ask trainees to send anything confusing through **Feedback & roadmap**.
