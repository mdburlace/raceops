**At the Track → Gears & tires** has five tabs.

## Gear lists

Every team starts with the standard 10-spline quick-change chart (70 sets, with 4.12, 4.57 and 4.86 ring & pinions). For a midget, micro or late model:

1. Press **New gear list**.
2. Paste the chart into **Sets**, one set per line, e.g. `15A 21/22`.
3. Enter the ring & pinions as pinion and ring teeth.

Then set each car's gear list and ring & pinion; see [Add your cars](/help/cars).

## Gear chart & availability

The low and high final drive for every set and ring & pinion. Enter **Team qty** for the sets you own, and untick any car that can't use a set.

## Gear calculator

Pick the car and the current gear (or type its final drive) and rpm, then choose:

- **Hit a final drive:** ranks the sets closest to the ratio you want.
- **Change rpm:** enter `+200`, `-150` or a target such as `8200`; it works out the ratio needed and ranks the sets.
- **Try a gear set:** the new final drive, % change, rpm at the same speed, and mph.

Results can be limited to the sets that car can use, or cover every ring & pinion.

## Tyres and stagger

**Tires** holds each tyre: ID, brand, compound, size, measured **circumference**, durometer, wheel, laps and heat cycles. Press **New tire** to add one.

**Stagger combos** uses the circumferences to show the stagger from each pair of rear tyres.
