**Business → Marketing** has three tabs: **Results posts**, **Media library** and **Merchandise**.

## Results posts

1. After a meeting, press **New results post** and pick the event (or use **Results post** on your race-night card).
2. Tag the sponsors to thank.
3. Press **Draft from result** — it writes the post from the sessions' finishing positions. With the AI add-on, **Draft with AI** writes a fuller one from the same facts. Change it as you like.
4. The marketing manager checks it and presses **Approve**, then **Save**.
5. **Share…** opens your phone's share sheet (on a computer it copies the post). Post it, then press **Mark as posted** and paste the link.

Posts marked **Posted** count towards each tagged sponsor's posts-per-event promise.

## Media library

**Add photo** for each shot: the caption, event, car, driver and the **Sponsors shown** (and the logo position). Add the photo itself under **Photos & files**. Tick **OK to publish** when it can go on the sponsor's page. Filter the library by sponsor to find their shots.

## Merchandise

Keep a stock count for each item and size, with a **Reorder at** level — low stock shows in Reminders. Sell through your online shop (Square, Shopify or similar) and paste its link on the item.

**Who can do this:** roles with Marketing → edit (the built-in Marketing Manager). Marketing must be in your team's plan; drafting with AI needs the AI add-on.
