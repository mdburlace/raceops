1. Go to **Engine Shop → Customers** and press **New customer**.
2. Fill in the name and contact details, then press **Save**.
3. On the customer's page, press **Add engine**. The engine is an [asset](/help/assets) with this customer as its **owner**.

A customer's page shows their contact details, their **Engines** and their **Jobs**.

**Engine Shop → Customer engines** lists every customer engine with its lifecycle bar. Open an engine to see its jobs, build sheets and **Customer access** (sharing).

**Changing an engine's owner** turns off its engine page link and ends any team link. The previous owner's team keeps its copy of the history.
