The built-in **Transport** role runs [Transport](/help/transport) and can edit assets and maintenance, so the hauler and trailer get their service schedules.

## Before a meeting

1. Plan the trip: **Business → Transport → New trip**.
2. Make the load list from the event and your template, and tick items **Loaded** as they go on.
3. Do the **pre-trip check** and tick **OK to travel**.

## At the track and after

- Tick **At track** as items come off, and **Back home** after.
- Record fuel and tolls on the trip, then **Record as a travel expense** (if your team uses Finance).

Your race-night card on Home links to the load list and trip plan. Reminders warns you about licence, registration and inspection dates.
