The owner doesn't need a RaceOps account for this.

## Create the link

1. Open the customer engine (**Engine Shop → Customer engines**, or from the customer).
2. Under **Customer access → Engine page link**, press **Create engine page link**.
3. Press **Copy link** and send it by text or email. **Open** shows exactly what they'll see.

## What the owner sees

- The engine, the owner, the service interval and the last service date.
- Every build: specs, clearances and torque figures.
- The work history: what was asked for, findings, work done, recommendations, and parts and labour.
- The engine tunes you share: the current version's pill settings, ending tune and fuel system. Untick **Share with the engine’s owner** on a tune to keep it to yourself. See [Engine tune sheets](/help/engine-tunes).
- **Prices**, only on jobs where you ticked **Show prices to the customer**.

They never see your internal notes, parts costs or margins. The panel shows how many times the link has been viewed.

## Replace or turn off

- **Replace link** makes a new link; the old one stops working.
- **Turn off** stops the link altogether.

Changing the engine's owner, or deleting the engine, turns the link off automatically.

Sharing is free. Your plan may limit how many engines are shared at once, counting engines with a page link, a [team link](/help/link-owner-team) or both. Turn sharing off on an old engine to free a place.
