## When an engine comes in

1. **Customer.** Find them under **Engine Shop → Customers**, or press **New customer**. [Customers](/help/customers)
2. **Engine.** On the customer, **Add engine** if it's new to you.
3. **Job.** **New job** on the engine: job type, what was asked for, date due, and who it's assigned to. Status **Booked in**. [Jobs](/help/shop-jobs)

## While you work

- Move the status along: *In progress*, *Waiting on parts*, *Ready for pickup*.
- Add **parts** and **labour** as you go. A blank labour rate uses the shop rate.
- Record findings, work done and recommendations.
- **Teardown** on the engine's card as it comes apart: plugs, chambers, oil filter, springs, valves, bores, mains, rods, pistons, crank and crack tests. It flags what's over your limits, points to the root cause and suggests what to reuse, machine or replace. [Engine teardown & pre-check](/help/engine-teardown)
- **New build sheet** on the job: specs, measurements, clearances, torque and dyno. The **Clearances & checks** card works itself out. [Engine build sheets](/help/engine-builds)

## Handing it back

1. **Job sheet / handover pack** prints or saves a PDF for the customer. [Job sheet and handover pack](/help/handover-pack)
2. Decide whether this job's prices show to the customer (**Show prices to the customer**).
3. Set the status to **Collected** when it leaves.

## Keeping the owner informed

- [Share an engine page](/help/share-engine-page): a link with no login, showing the build and work history.
- [Link it to their RaceOps team](/help/link-owner-team): they see your history, and you see their laps since the service. If they turn it on, their laps count on your lifecycle bars.
