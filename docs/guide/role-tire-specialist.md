The built-in **Tire Specialist** role edits **Gears & tires** and only the **Tires** and **Wheels** sections of each setup sheet.

## The tyre sheet

The crew chief sets **Tyre targets** on the sheet: target cold pressures, stagger and rear wheel spacing.

1. On race night, tap **Tyre sheet** on your Home card (or open the session's sheet).
2. Set the tyres and wheels, and fill in **Pressure cold**, the stagger and the spacing under **Tires** and **Wheels**.
3. The **Tyre sheet** table under Tires shows each target beside what was set, and flags anything off target.
4. Press **Save**.

The stagger calculator is in [Gears & tires](/help/gears).

## The tyre work log

Every tyre in **Gears & tires → Tires** has a **Work log**: each time it's mounted, cut, siped, ground or measured, add a row with the date, the work, circumference and durometer readings, and who did it.
