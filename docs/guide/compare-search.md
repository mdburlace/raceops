## Compare

Put 2–4 sheets side by side with the differences highlighted. Turn on **Only differences** to hide what's the same. Start from:

- an event: **Compare sessions**;
- **Setups:** tick up to four sheets and press **Compare**.

On the compare page, **+ Add a sheet to compare…** adds another. Engine build sheets compare the same way: tick them in Engine Builds and press **Compare builds**.

## Search conditions and feedback

**Setups → Search conditions & feedback** filters every sheet by:

- track condition and car behaviour tags, from the driver, the crew lead or either;
- track and car;
- words in the comments.

The matching words are highlighted. Tick results and press **Compare**. This answers "what did we run last time it was slick here?"

**Baselines** on the same page lists your saved starting setups.
