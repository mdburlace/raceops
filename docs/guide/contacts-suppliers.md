- **Contacts.** People: name, organisation, position, phones, emails, website and address. **Call** and **Email** buttons work straight from the page.
  - **Shared** contacts (e.g. track promoters) are kept by the platform admin and seen by every team.
  - **Team** contacts are private to your team.
  - **Tags** say what a contact is to the team — Sponsor, Supplier, Engine customer, Promoter, Hospitality guest, Transport driver, Media, Official. One contact can have several, so nobody keeps a second address book.
  - Tick **Agreed to be contacted** (and the date) where privacy law needs a record of consent, e.g. GDPR in the UK and Europe.
- **Suppliers.** Per team: category, account number, phone, email, address and their people.
- **Roles.** Link a contact to a track, supplier, series, event or car, with a role on each, e.g. *Track promoter*, *Race director*, *Sales* or *Accounts*. You can also type your own role. The same person can hold different roles on different records.

Go to **Team → Contacts** or **Team → Suppliers**, then press **New contact** or **New supplier**.
