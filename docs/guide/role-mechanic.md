You keep the car right. The built-in Mechanic role can edit checks, maintenance, assets, dyno runs and engine builds, and can read setups.

## At the track

- **Between sessions:** run a [between-round check](/help/between-round-checks). Tick pressures, lugs, wear, fluids, leaks, shocks and the rest, and note anything that needs attention.
- **Setups:** read the crew chief's sheet for the next session, e.g. pressures, stagger and gear. **Compare sessions** shows what changed.

## In the workshop

1. **Home** and the **Workshop** hub list schedules that are due and parts near their limit.
2. Do the car's **checklist**: Weekly, Every 2nd race or Monthly / 4th race. [Checklists and schedules](/help/maintenance)
3. When a scheduled job is done, press **Done today** on the schedule.
4. **Parts:**
   - Record measurements (e.g. bar rate, pad thickness) on the asset so they chart over time.
   - Update a part's status when it goes out for service or is retired. [Assets](/help/assets)
5. **Shocks:** after a rebuild or re-valve, record a dyno run. Import the printout or an Excel file. [Shock and bar dyno](/help/dyno)

## New parts

Add new shocks with their **position** and **adjustable type**, so they show at the right corner, with the right adjuster boxes, on setup sheets.
