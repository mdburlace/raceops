1. Go to **Team → Members & invites**.
2. In **Team type & engine shop**, set:
   - **Labour rate.** Per hour, before tax. A labour line with no rate uses this.
   - **Tax rate** and **tax name**, e.g. 10% GST.
   - **Prices include tax.** Tick it if the prices you enter already include tax.
   - **Job number prefix**, e.g. *J* gives J-0001, J-0002… Numbers count up automatically.
   - **Terms.** Printed at the bottom of every job sheet and handover pack.
3. Press **Save**.

**Who can change them:** the owner, or a role with **Edit team settings**.

RaceOps records invoice numbers but doesn't produce invoices; those still come from your accounting software.
