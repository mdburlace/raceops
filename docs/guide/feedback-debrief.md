Feedback is what makes the [setup advisor](/help/setup-advisor) and search useful. Record it every session, even when it's brief.

## On the sheet

- **Track conditions.** Quick picks such as *Tacky*, *Slick*, *Rubbered up* or *Big cushion*, plus notes.
- **Driver** and **Crew lead.** How the car behaved, e.g. *Loose exit*, *Tight entry* or *No drive off*, plus their own comments.
- **Changes for next session.** What you plan to change.

You can type your own words if the quick picks don't fit. Your platform admin manages the standard quick-pick lists.

## Voice debrief (AI add-on)

1. On a sheet, press **Voice debrief**.
2. Choose **Driver** or **Crew lead**.
3. Tap **Talk** (or use the keyboard's microphone) and describe the session.
4. Press **Fill feedback**. It turns what was said into condition and behaviour picks, tidied comments, track notes and changes for next session.
5. Check them, then **Apply**.

Offline, **Save to comments as is** keeps the words to tidy later.
