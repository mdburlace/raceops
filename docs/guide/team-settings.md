**Team** in the sidebar (Team Management) opens the team hub. **Members & invites** holds the team's administration:

- **Licence.** Your plan, seats and licence end date. After the end date the team is **read-only**: everyone can still sign in and read, but changes are refused until the licence is renewed. A red banner shows when this happens.
- **AI assistant.** Whether AI is on for the team, and this month's spend against the allowance. See [AI assistant](/help/ai-assistant).
- **Team type & engine shop.** See [Choose your team type](/help/team-type) and [Shop settings](/help/shop-settings).
- **Team settings.** Rename the team.
- **Invite someone** and **Members.** See [Invite your crew](/help/members-invites).

Team settings need the **Edit team settings** right, which owners always have. For every setting in one place, see [All settings and options](/help/all-settings).
