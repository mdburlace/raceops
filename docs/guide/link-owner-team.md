If the engine's owner uses RaceOps for their race team, link the engine to their team.

- **They see** your build and job history, read-only.
- **You see** only the laps and sessions run on that engine since its last service, and when. You never see their setups, tracks or results.

## Send the invite

1. Open the customer engine.
2. Under **Customer access → Link to the owner's RaceOps team**, press **Invite the owner's team**.
3. Send them the link, or the invite code. They can also enter the code under *Engine Builds → Link an engine from your builder*.

Someone on their team opens it, signs in and picks which of their engines it is. **Cancel invite** withdraws it before it's used.

## Their laps on your service tracker

The **team** decides, per engine, whether their laps count on your service tracker; see [Engines from your engine builder](/help/linked-engines). Customer access shows whether they've turned it on:

- **On.** The engine's lifecycle bar (engine page, customer page, asset list and Home alerts) includes their laps since the last service.
- **Off.** You still see their lap count in Customer access, but the lifecycle bar uses only the laps you record.

## End the link

Either side can press **End link**. Each keeps its own records, and the team keeps a copy of the build history as it was that day. Changing the engine's owner ends the link automatically.

Your plan may limit how many engines are shared at once (page links and team links together). Ask your platform admin if you hit the limit.
