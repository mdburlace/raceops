**Time:** about 70 minutes (do the exercises for your job). **You need:** the practice team. The owner can do every exercise; otherwise sign in with the role named.

**The story:** it's club night at Valley Speedway. The crew each have a job, the business side keeps the money and sponsors right, and round 9 is next week.

## Exercise 1: Put yourself on the crew (owner or crew chief, 5 min)

1. Open **At the Track → Events → Valley Speedway — Club night**.
2. Under **Crew**, press **Edit crew**. Add a row: pick yourself and type your job, e.g. *Car chief*.
3. Press **Save**, then go to **Home**.

**When it's right:** Home shows **Race night: Valley Speedway — Club night** with *You're on the crew as Car chief* and a **Change list** button.

## Exercise 2: Call changes and tick them off (crew chief, then car chief, 10 min)

![Video: Race night: call the changes and tick them off](video/training-change-list.webm)

1. On the event, add a **Feature** session for Car 17. It copies Heat 1.
2. Change **Stagger cold** to *5* and **Wing angle** to *12*.
3. Scroll to **Change list** and press **Add changes from this sheet**. Press **Save**.
4. As the car chief (or yourself): open the sheet from **Change list** on Home, tick **Done** beside each change, and press **Save**.

**When it's right:** each change shows a tick with your name and the time.

**Check yourself:** a car chief can't change the stagger itself — why not? (Part rights: only the change list is open to them.)

## Exercise 3: The tyre sheet (crew chief and tyre specialist, 5 min)

![Video: The tyre sheet and a tyre's work log](video/training-tyre-sheet.webm)

1. On the Feature sheet, under **Tyre targets**, set target cold pressures, e.g. RR *15*.
2. Under **Tires**, set **Pressure cold** RR to *16* and save.

**When it's right:** the **Tyre sheet** table under Tires shows RR pressure *+1* against the target.

## Exercise 4: The driver's debrief (driver, 5 min)

1. Sign in with the **Driver** role and open the Heat 1 sheet.
2. Fill in **Track conditions & car feedback** — conditions, how the car felt, comments — and press **Save**.

**When it's right:** the sheet says you can change *Track conditions & car feedback*, and only that section has boxes to type in.

## Exercise 5: Approve a parts order (crew chief, 5 min)

![Video: Approve a parts order and see it in the meeting's profit and loss](video/training-parts-order.webm)

1. Go to **Business → Parts orders**.
2. Beside *RR shock rebuild kit*, press **Approve**.

**When it's right:** it shows **Approved** and is gone from **Reminders**. A mechanic sees the order but not its cost.

## Exercise 6: An expense and the event's profit and loss (business manager, 10 min)

1. **Business → Finance → New expense**. Category *Tyres*, amount *900*, event *Valley Speedway — Club night*. Save.
2. Open **Event P&L** and pick tonight's event.
3. Open **Budget**. Find *Tyres* under **Parts, maintenance & consumables**, then look at **Contingency reserve** and the income expected against received.

**When it's right:** tonight's P&L shows the tyres under Costs. The budget shows *Tyres* over its warning level, and Reminders lists it. The contingency reserve (15% of the budget) shows the crash repair already taken out of it.

**Check yourself:** the car hits the wall and needs a new front axle. Where does the cost go? (Category **Crash & failure repairs** — it comes out of the contingency reserve. Or tick **Paid from the contingency reserve** on an expense in another category.)

## Exercise 7: Load the hauler (transport, 10 min)

![Video: Load the hauler for round 9](video/training-load-list.webm)

1. **Business → Transport → Load lists → New load list**. Pick round 9 (*Lakeside Raceway — Club Championship*) and the vehicle *Hauler*.
2. Pick the template *Sprint car race weekend* and press **Add the event's car, engines and the template**. Save.
3. Tick **Loaded** on three items and save.

**When it's right:** the list shows *3 loaded* and each tick shows who and when.

## Exercise 8: A sponsor's deliverables and page (partnerships, 10 min)

![Video: A sponsor's promises, guest check-in, payments and in kind](video/training-sponsor.webm)

1. **Business → Partnerships** and open *Valley Tyres*.
2. Read **What we promised**: the logo, a post per event and four hospitality passes.
3. Go to **Hospitality**, pick tonight's event and **Check in** *Chris Lee*.
4. Back on *Valley Tyres*, press **Make a sponsor page link**, then **Open**.

5. Under **Relationship**, add a **Call** today: *Sent the season report*, follow up in a week. Press **Add to history**.
6. Press **Edit**, set the *Suit / helmet* promise's **Status** to **Deployed** and save.

**When it's right:** hospitality passes show *2 / 4*, and the sponsor page shows the deal without its value. Your call is at the top of the interaction history, the follow-up shows on the sponsor and in Reminders, and the suit promise shows **Done**.

**Check yourself:** you're about to sign *Lakeside Motors*, who sell tyres too. What warns you? (Give them the industry *Tyres*: *Valley Tyres* is exclusive, so both pages show **Exclusivity clash**.)

## Exercise 9: A results post (marketing, 5 min)

1. **Business → Marketing → New results post**. Pick the Hilltop round and tag *Valley Tyres*.
2. Press **Draft from result**, then **Approve**, then **Save**.

**When it's right:** the post shows **Approved** and **Share…** is offered.

## Exercise 10: Sponsor payments, in kind and crew pay (business manager, 10 min)

1. **Business → Partnerships**, open *Valley Tyres* and find **Payments & in kind**. *INV-101* is received; *INV-102* is invoiced and due in 10 days.
2. Press **Mark received** on *INV-102*.
3. Under **In kind**, press **Record in Finance** on the *Hoosier race tyres*.
4. **Business → Finance → Contracts → New contract**: *Crew pay — you*, kind **Crew / contractor**, **Team member** yourself, **Per diem (each day)** *50*, **Pay per meeting** *100*. Save.
5. Open *Valley Speedway — Club night* (you're on its crew from Exercise 1). Under **Crew**, press **Add crew costs to Finance**.

**When it's right:** *Valley Tyres* shows nothing outstanding and the income lists both payments. **In-kind sponsorship** income and a *Tyres* cost of the same amount are in Finance. Two bills to pay — your per diem and pay — are in **Expenses** and **Reminders**.

**Check yourself:** why record in-kind tyres in Finance if the income and cost cancel out? (So the budget shows what the season really costs — the tyres would cost that without the sponsor — and the sponsor gets the credit for them.)

## Sign-off

Show your trainer your race-night card, a ticked change list, an approved parts order and a sponsor page.
