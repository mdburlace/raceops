## New features
- **Team photo.** **Team → Team profile → Add team photo** adds a team picture, car shot or logo. It shows on the Team page and beside the team's name at the top of the menu.
- **Change the team photo.** A new right under **Roles → Administration**, so the owner can let someone else look after the team photo. The built-in **Marketing Manager** has it in new teams.
- **Member photos.** Add yours on **Profile & device → Your photo**. People who can manage members add one for anyone in the team with **Add photo** under their name. Photos show beside names under **Members** and at the bottom of the menu.
- **Say it.** On the quick add screen, press **🎤 Say it** and say the number (*one and three eighths*, *fourteen point five*).
- **Last session.** The quick add screen shows the same box's value on the session before; tap it to use it.

## Improvements
- **Turns** has − and + buttons that move it half a turn.
- Photos are made smaller on the phone before they're sent, so they upload quickly on a slow connection.

## Notes
- Existing teams: tick **Change the team photo** on the roles that should have it (such as Marketing Manager) in **Roles**. The owner and roles with **Edit team settings** can always change it.
- A team backup includes the team photo. Member photos are in full server backups (the uploads folder).
- **Say it** works in Chrome, Edge and Safari, not Firefox, and asks to use the microphone the first time.
- The server adds a database column for member photos when it starts (migration 020). Nothing to set up.
- Close and reopen the app on each phone to get the update.
