## Improvements
- **A line for each day in lists.** A multi-day event in **Events**, **Calendar**, the shared calendars, a series' rounds, a track's events, **At the Track** and the printed and published calendar has a line for each day under it.
    - Each line shows the day's date, its name (or *Day 1 of 2*) and its start time.
    - A line down the left joins the days to their event.
    - A rained-out or cancelled day is crossed out. Days already past are greyed, and today's day says *today*.
- On the **Calendar** and a series' rounds, tap a day's line to open the event on that day.
- Shared calendar dates and calendar entries show **2 days** (or however many) beside their name, like **Events** does.
