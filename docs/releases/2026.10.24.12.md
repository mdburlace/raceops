## New features
- **Season forecast** (Finance → Forecast): by category, spent so far + parts on order + the planned meetings left × what each category has cost per meeting, against the budget, with a clear "over" or "under" and by how much. The income side adds what's still to come and prize money per meeting, for a season net forecast.
- **Cash flow — the next 8 weeks:** money in and out week by week from what's due and the meetings coming up. Add **Cash in the bank** to the budget for a running balance and a warning if it would go below zero.
- **What's owed** (Finance → Owed): sponsor instalments and income not yet received, and unpaid bills, each by age (not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, over 90 days late), plus parts on order that haven't been billed.
- **Depreciation:** set an asset to depreciate on a straight line (over its useful life in years) or per lap (against its life limit), down to an end value. Its page shows its book value.
- **Disposals:** record when an asset is sold, scrapped, returned or written off and what you got; the gain or loss is worked out against its book value.
- **Asset register** (Finance → Assets): cost, depreciation, book value and this season's depreciation for each asset, and disposals with their gain or loss.

## Improvements
- **Season P&L** shows depreciation and gains or losses on disposals under "Not paid in cash", for a net after depreciation.
- The practice team's hauler and engine depreciate, a sold shock shows a gain or loss, and the budget has cash in the bank.

## Notes
- Forecasts and depreciation are estimates for planning; your accountant's tax depreciation may differ.
- Book values, end values, sale values and cash in the bank only show to roles with **See costs, prices and amounts**.
- Close and reopen the app on each phone to get the update.
