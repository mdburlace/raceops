## New features
- **What happened each day of a multi-day event.** Each day's tab on the event has **what happened** for that day: **Raced the feature**, **Did not make the feature**, **Withdrew** (with the reason) or **Did not race**, the result in a few words (e.g. *Prelim feature 5th*) and notes.
- **Overall result from the days.** Under **Plan & result**, the meeting's result is worked out from its days until you pick one yourself: raced a feature on any day counts as **Raced the feature**, then a withdrawal, then a missed feature.

## Improvements
- Each day's result shows on its line under the event in **Events**, the **Calendar** and the other lists, and as chips under **Plan & result**.
- With **Show results** on, the published calendar page shows each day's result. Withdraw reasons and day notes are never published.
- A day's result stays with your team. It's never sent to the shared calendars, and updates from a shared calendar keep it.
