## New features
- **Quick add screen.** Long-press (or right-click) a number box on a track or workshop setup sheet for a big keypad, the values your team uses most in that box, and **+ 1/16** to **+ 15/16** for measurements in inches.
- **Angles: Pick and the angle finder.** Wing angles, chassis rake and tilt and birdcage angles can be picked from a list, or read with **Angle finder** by laying your phone on the wing, frame rail or birdcage. **Zero** levels it for a car that isn't sitting level.
- **Turns.** A new four-corner box for the torsion stop adjuster turns.
- **Chassis rake and tilt, RR radius rod (front mount), birdcage angles (LR and RR) and the Jacobs ladder** (length, upper and lower strap lengths, upper and lower chassis mounts, birdcage mount) in **Chassis**, and on the workshop setup.
- **Highest RPM (overall)** in **Race**, beside **High RPM (straights)** and **Low RPM (corners)**.

## Improvements
- **Blocked** is now **Block height**, in inches.
- A track sheet started from a workshop setup takes its setup block heights as **Block height** and its turns as **Turns** (turns used to go into Blocked), plus the new chassis measurements.
- The practice team's sheets have block heights, turns and rpm readings, and the crew chief training has an exercise for the quick add screen and angle finder.

## Notes
- Every team's setup sheet and workshop setup forms get the new boxes when the server is updated. A team that renamed **Blocked** or the rpm boxes will see the new names; rename them again in **Forms** if you prefer yours.
- Values already entered in Blocked stay as they are, now under Block height.
- The angle finder needs a phone or tablet with a tilt sensor. On an iPhone, allow motion and orientation access the first time.
- Close and reopen the app on each phone to get the update.
