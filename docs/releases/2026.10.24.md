## New features
- **Multi-day events:** tick **Runs over more than one day** on an event or shared calendar date, then set each day: its date, a name such as *Night 1* or *Finals*, a start time, and whether it was rained out.
    - The event page has a tab for each day.
    - Setup sheets pick their day.
    - The calendar, month view and iCal feeds show each day.

## Improvements
- The month view marks only the days a meeting runs. A rained-out day is crossed out.
- The clash check counts only the days a meeting runs.
- On the calendar's quick edit, a new date moves all the days, and the end date adds or takes off days.
- Ask RaceOps, Analytics, engine laps for your engine builder, and the weather on a setup sheet use each day's own date.
- A link in the guide to a section opens the article at that section.

## Notes
- Older events with a date and an end date open with one row for each day.
- A day that has setup sheets can't be removed. Move the sheets to another day first.
- Close and reopen the app on each phone to get the update. Until a phone updates, it shows the days as plain text.
