## New features
- **Feedback & roadmap** in the sidebar:
    - Report a problem or ask for an improvement or a new feature.
    - Follow what happens under **My submissions**, and vote on the **Roadmap**.
- Platform admins sort everything into **Now**, **Next** or **Later**. They can send work to Claude as a GitHub issue or as a ready-made brief.

## Improvements
- **Bars, tyres, wheels and wings from inventory** on the setup sheet. Each corner lists the items meant for it first. **Enter manually…** and **+ Save to inventory** cover anything that isn't listed.
- The **Wing** section has a **Top wing** and a **Front wing**, and the front wing has its own wicker.
- Event lists show the round and track first, e.g. *Rd 3 · Valley Speedway*.
- **Save** and **Cancel** are at the top of the calendar date form.

## Notes
- **Send to Claude** as a GitHub issue needs `GITHUB_TOKEN` and `GITHUB_REPO`. Without them you can still copy the brief.
