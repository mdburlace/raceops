## New features
- **Events in more than one series.** An event or shared calendar date can count towards as many series as you like, each with its own round, under **Also counts towards**. Each series lists it in its rounds, and lists show every series it's in.
- **Add events to a series.** A series' page has **Add events to this series**: tick your events, set each one's prefix and round, and add them in one go. **Take out** removes one from that series only.
- **Add shared dates to a series (platform admin).** **Edit series** on a shared series now has **Dates in this series** and **Add shared dates**, so you can link dates to a series without opening each one. Teams' entries follow.
- **Round prefixes.** Next to the round number, pick **Round**, **Night** or **Prelude**, type your own, or leave it blank. Platform admins can change the list in **Pick-lists → Round prefixes**.
- **Lock a finished setup sheet.** **Lock sheet** makes a sheet read-only for everyone, the owner included, and stops it being deleted. The owner, and roles given **Unlock locked setup sheets**, can unlock it.

## Improvements
- Lists, the calendar, series and track pages, the shared calendars, the public calendar and iCal feeds show the prefix and round, e.g. *Night 2* or *Prelude*, instead of *Rd 2*.
- Locked sheets show 🔒 in an event's session list.

## Notes
- The built-in **Crew Chief** role gets **Unlock locked setup sheets** for new teams. For an existing team, the owner ticks it on the roles that should have it in **Roles → Other rights**.
- Existing rounds show as *Round N* until you pick another prefix.
- Close and reopen the app on each phone to get the update.
