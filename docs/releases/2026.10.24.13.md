## New features
- **Engine teardown & pre-check** (**Teardown** on an engine's card, under Engine Builds or Engine Shop → Build sheets), in four parts:
  - **Forensic clean & visual log:** each cylinder's spark plug and combustion chamber, the oil filter (ferrous and non-ferrous metal and what it looks like) and a fuel dilution test of the drained oil.
  - **High-stress valvetrain:** every spring's seat and open pressure against a new spring, valve length for stretch, guide clearance, and retainers and keepers.
  - **Rotating assembly & block tolerances:** bores at three depths with a torque plate (taper and out-of-round worked out), main housing bores and the back of the main bearings, rod big ends, rod bolts and rod crack tests, ring vertical clearance, piston skirts and wrist pins, and the crank snout, keyways and oil pump drive.
  - **Crack testing & stress mapping:** Magnaflux or dye penetrant on the main webs, chambers, crank fillets and snout; deck flatness; deck height side to side.
- **Findings as you go:** readings over your limits are flagged in red under each part, and readings close to a limit in amber.
- **Findings & decisions:** what the evidence points to (detonation, lean, oil starvation, fuel in the oil, metal fatigue, over-revving…), a suggested reuse, machine or replace for each part, and **Fill in suggested decisions** for the decisions table. Record the root cause and the tuning limits for the next build.
- **Start the rebuild sheet** from a teardown. The build sheet shows **Teardown before this build** with the root cause, the parts machined or replaced and the tuning limits.
- **Print** a teardown with its findings, or **Print a blank teardown sheet** from the engine's card for the bench.

## Improvements
- A new teardown is of the engine's latest build and copies the limits and new-part figures from the engine's last teardown.
- The practice team's customer engine, *Hart 410*, has a teardown to learn on, with a new engine builder exercise.

## Notes
- Anyone whose role can edit engine builds can do teardowns; roles that can view engine builds can read them. No role changes are needed.
- Existing teams get the teardown form when the server restarts after the update.
