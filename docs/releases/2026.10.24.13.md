## New features
- **Ten more training videos**, each with a voice-over and the caption bar:
    - **Everyone:** working without signal.
    - **Race night:** calling changes and ticking them off; the tyre sheet and a tyre's work log.
    - **Crew chief:** quick add, half turns, the angle Pick list and the angle finder.
    - **Mechanic:** sending a shock for a rebuild and recording it back.
    - **Business:** approving a parts order and seeing it in the meeting's profit and loss; loading the hauler; a sponsor's promises, guest check-in, payments and in kind.
    - **Team owner:** making a role; backing up the team and putting it back.

## Improvements
- **Help & guide → Training → Training videos** groups the videos by who they're for, and each video sits at the top of the exercise it shows.

## Notes
- The videos need an internet connection, like the others.
