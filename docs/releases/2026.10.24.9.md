## New features
- **Narrated training videos.** Each training video has a voice-over explaining each step as it happens, and the caption bar at the bottom of the picture as before. The player's captions button (**CC**) shows every word the voice says.
- **Training videos page.** **Help & guide → Training → Training videos** has every video in one place, by role, with a link to each role's exercises. The videos still appear at the top of their role's exercises too.

## Improvements
- The training start page and trainer's guide link to the new page.

## Notes
- The videos need an internet connection. Like before, they aren't kept on phones for offline use.
- For whoever records the videos: `tools/training_videos.py` now makes the voice-over with Amazon Polly (`--tts polly`, voice Matthew, which the videos use), Azure AI Speech (`--tts azure`) or Piper, free and offline (`--tts piper`, with a voice whose licence allows commercial use). Keys stay in environment variables (`AZURE_SPEECH_KEY` and `AZURE_SPEECH_REGION`, or the usual AWS credentials). See the script's header.
- Close and reopen the app on each phone to get the update.
