## New features
- **Budget in groups.** Finance → Budget shows spend against budget under Vehicle & hardware, Parts maintenance & consumables, Personnel & payroll, Logistics & travel, Event & administrative, Marketing & hospitality, Contingency and Other, with a total for each group.
- **New expense categories:** Car purchase & lease, Per diems & catering, Track hire & testing, and Crash & failure repairs.
- **Contingency reserve.** Set aside a share of the budget (10–20% is usual) for crash repairs and mechanical failures. Crash & failure repairs, and expenses ticked **Paid from the contingency reserve**, draw it down; the Budget tab shows what's used and left.
- **Income budget.** List the income you expect by kind; the Budget tab compares it with what's been received.
- **New income kinds:** Seat fee / driver contribution, Series stipend / subsidy, and In-kind sponsorship.
- **Sponsor record:** billing contact, website & social links, industry, **Open to renewing?** with when it was last discussed, exclusivity and its terms, and activation notes.
- **Interaction history.** Log meetings, calls and emails on a sponsor's page. A follow-up date shows on the sponsor, in the pipeline and in Reminders until a later entry is added.
- **Exclusivity clash warning** when two sponsors in the same industry are in play at the same time and either is exclusive.
- **More kinds of promise** — suit or helmet, pit and trailer signage, website, newsletter, social shout-out, PA announcement, podium or award, car display — each with a status (Planned, Designed, Approved by sponsor, Deployed), a status date and a proof photo.

## Improvements
- A deployed promise counts as done on the sponsor's page, the pipeline, the season report and the sponsor's own page, and drops off Reminders.
- The new categories appear under **Team → Region & currency → accounting export settings** for their account codes.
- The practice team's budget has a contingency reserve, an income budget and a crash repair; *Valley Tyres* has an interaction history, exclusivity and a suit promise.

## Notes
- Every team's expense, income, budget and sponsor forms get the new fields when the server is updated. Existing budgets have no reserve or income budget until you add them.
- Categories you typed yourself count under **Other** in the budget groups.
- Close and reopen the app on each phone to get the update.
