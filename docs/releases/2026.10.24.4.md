## New features
- **Team roles and the Business area.** New built-in roles for race night and the business side, and five new modules under **Business**.
- **New built-in roles:** **Driver** (fills in Track conditions & car feedback — the debrief — and nothing else), **Car Chief** (Mechanic plus ticking off the change list), **Tire Specialist** (tyres, and the tyre and wheel sections of the sheet), **Viewer** (read-only), **Transport**, **Business Manager**, **Partnerships Manager** and **Marketing Manager**.
    - **Add standard roles** (Team → Roles & permissions) adds the ones your team doesn't have yet.
    - **More than one role:** **+ Also…** beside a member gives them another role; they get everything any of their roles allows.
    - **Other rights** on a role: part rights for setup sheet sections, **Approve or decline parts orders**, and **See costs, prices and amounts**.
- **Race night:** an event's **Crew** list (who's going and their job) puts a **Race night** card on each person's Home with the screens for their job.
- **Setup sheets:** a **Change list** the crew chief fills (or **Add changes from this sheet**) and the car chief ticks off, and **Tyre targets** with a tyre sheet showing what was set against them.
- **Tyres:** a work log on each tyre — mounted, cut, siped, circumference and durometer.
- **Parts orders** with an approval step, then into Finance as an expense.
- **Finance:** season budget with warnings, expenses (receipts read by the AI, tax by region, any currency), income, contracts, a **profit and loss for every event** (including tyre, engine and parts wear from laps) and the season, and an **export for Xero, QuickBooks Online, MYOB, Sage or a universal CSV**, the same in every region.
- **Transport:** trip plans, load lists built from the event and a template and ticked off with no signal, pre-trip checks, driver licences and vehicles (assets with the category Vehicle).
- **Partnerships:** the sponsor pipeline, deliverables that count their own evidence, paint schemes and decal stock, hospitality guests with check-in, a printable season report and proposal, and a no-login sponsor page.
- **Marketing:** results posts drafted from the result (or with AI), approved, then shared; the media library; merchandise stock.
- **Reminders** (Business → Reminders, and **Coming up** on Home): licences, vehicle registration and inspections, bills, contracts, sponsor renewals and deliverables, low stock, budget warnings and parts orders to approve.
- **Region & currency** (Team → Members & invites): Australia, New Zealand, the United States, the United Kingdom or Europe sets tax, business numbers, licence classes, work-hour rules, inspections and accounting codes.

## Improvements
- The event page shows the crew, trip and load lists, parts orders, money, guests, photos and posts for the meeting, each with **+ Add**.
- Contacts have **tags** (Sponsor, Supplier, Promoter…) and a consent record.
- The published calendar page can list your signed sponsors.
- Drivers can use **Voice debrief** with only the debrief right.

## Notes
- New teams get every new role. Existing teams: press **Team → Roles & permissions → Add standard roles**. The old **Driver / Viewer** role stays as it is; give drivers the new **Driver** role to let them fill in the debrief.
- Finance, Transport, Partnerships and Marketing are licence features. They're in the built-in **Pro** plan; a plan your platform admin has changed needs them ticked in **Platform admin → Plans & allocation**, or turned on per team.
- Parts orders need no licence feature. Costs on parts orders and amounts in Finance only show to roles with **See costs, prices and amounts** (Owner, Crew Chief, Business Manager and Partnerships Manager have it).
- Direct connections to accounting software and enterprise systems (NetSuite, Dynamics 365 Business Central, Sage Intacct, SAP Business One) are planned, not in this release.
- Close and reopen the app on each phone to get the update.
