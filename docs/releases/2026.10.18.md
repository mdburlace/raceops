## New features
- **Setup sheets, maintenance and assets** for each car, saved on your phone and synced when you have signal. Runs as a Portainer stack.
- **Four areas:** **At the Track**, **In the Workshop**, **Engine Builds** and **Team Management**, each with its own access in roles. Team plans decide which features a team has.
- **Between-round checks, gears and tyres, the shock and bar dyno, engine build sheets, reports, Crew Chief stats and performance reviews.**
- **Shock dyno sweeps** for non-adjustable to four-way shocks, with readings for each adjuster setting. Paste Kenco, Slade and Roehrig printouts straight in.
- **Gears:** a standard gear chart for every team, extra gear lists, each car's ring and pinion and the gears it can run, and a gear calculator.
- **Track directory** of 379 tracks in Australia, New Zealand, the USA and Canada. Add contacts and suppliers with their roles, and race series with their rounds.
- **Events** with one car each, the track picked from the directory, and sessions added one at a time in running order. Multi-day meetings get a tab for each day.
- **New setup sheets start from** the previous session, last time out, a saved baseline or blank. The final drive is worked out for you. Track conditions and how the car felt can be searched later.
- **Platform admin:** teams, licences, plans, users, the track directory, pick-lists, settings, an announcement banner and an audit log.
- **Sign-in protection:** an account is locked for a while after too many wrong passwords.
- **AI assistant** add-on: fill a form from a photo, a voice debrief after a session, and **Ask RaceOps** questions about your records.
- **Setup advisor:** suggests changes from what has worked for your team before and from tuning guides.

## Improvements
- New Zealand track list updated: 40 tracks, 32 of them new.

## Bug fixes
- Records could download in the wrong order when syncing.
- Licence end dates now save and show the date you set.
- A wrong password when accepting an invite no longer signs you out.

## Notes
- The stack needs `JWT_SECRET` and `POSTGRES_PASSWORD`. AI is optional and needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (and `ANTHROPIC_WORKSPACE_ID` if your key isn't limited to one workspace).
- The README covers publishing through pfSense with HTTPS, and `TRUST_PROXY`.
