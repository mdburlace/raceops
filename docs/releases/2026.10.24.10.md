## New features
- **Sponsor payment schedule.** List each instalment with its due date, amount and invoice. The sponsor's page shows scheduled, received, outstanding and overdue; **Mark received** records the income and links it.
- **In-kind contributions.** Goods and services a sponsor gives, with their fair value. **Record in Finance** adds the value as In-kind sponsorship income and as a matching cost, so the budget shows the real cost of the season.
- **Add to inventory.** On a saved expense or a received parts order, make the asset or tyre it bought, with its cost, supplier and date, linked back as the **Inventory item**.
- **Spent on this.** An asset's page shows what it was bought for and every expense and parts order linked to it — services, repairs, rebuilds.
- **Trip costs into Finance.** **Add to Finance** on a trip plan records the fuel and tolls as one Transport expense for the event, linked to the trip and the vehicle; **Update the expense** if they change.
- **Crew pay.** Crew / contractor and Driver contracts take a team member, a per diem and pay per meeting. **Add crew costs to Finance** on the event page drafts each person's per diem (× the meeting's days) and pay as bills to pay.

## Improvements
- Sponsor instalments not yet received show in Reminders a week before they're due.
- Expenses have **For (crew member)**; drafted crew costs fill it in and aren't added twice.
- Received in-kind items are listed in the sponsor's season report, without values.
- The practice team's *Valley Tyres* deal has a payment schedule (one instalment received, one invoiced) and tyres given in kind.

## Bug fixes
- The income budget on a season budget is now hidden, like the rest of the budget, from roles that can't see costs and amounts.

## Notes
- Payments, in-kind values and crew rates only show to roles with **See costs, prices and amounts**.
- Close and reopen the app on each phone to get the update.
