## New features
- **Share your series, tracks and major events** with every team. A platform admin approves each request. Approved records become the one shared copy, and later changes flow to every team.
- **Our notes** on shared series, tracks and dates, private to your team.
- **Follow** a series or track. **Calendar → Updates** tells you when dates are added, moved, cancelled or get results.
- **Clashes:** meetings on the same day in the same region.
- **Results** on shared dates, reported by teams.
- **Public page** and **Subscribe** link for every shared series and track.
- **For admins:**
    - Ask a team a question about a request.
    - Appoint a series' or track's own team as its **official**.
    - Add official iCal feeds.
    - Merge duplicates, and see history with restore.
- **Emails** about requests and followed calendars, if the server has email set up.

## Improvements
- **Choose rounds for a partial series** from every round in the series, with **Tick all to come** and **Clear**.

## Bug fixes
- After your role or your team's licence changed, the app could show **Synced** with some records missing. It now downloads everything again.

## Notes
- Email needs the `SMTP_*` settings and `APP_URL`. Official feeds are checked every `CALENDAR_FEED_HOURS` (12 by default).
- Teams' older date suggestions were turned into requests automatically.
- For whoever runs the server: `docs/design/DESIGN.md` describes the whole system, `ROLLOUT.md` covers releases, and `tests/e2e` holds the browser tests.
