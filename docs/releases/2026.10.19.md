## New features
- **Calendars:** plan each racing year under **Team Management → Calendar**.
    - Pull dates from shared series and track calendars, enter a full or part series, and mark meetings planned or tentative.
    - Record what happened, and print or publish the year.
    - Shared calendars have **Series**, **Tracks** and **Major events** views, and platform admins manage every shared date.
- **Engine shops:** a new team type with customers, jobs with parts and labour, and handover packs. Share an engine page with its owner, or link it to the owner's RaceOps team. The owner chooses whether their laps count on the shop's service tracker.
- **Calculators:** 47 calculators for tuning, chassis, gearing, the engine and the workshop. Many open from the field they fill.
- **Track weather:** pull the weather for a session, or work out ADR, density altitude and grains from your own readings.
- **Analytics:** results over time, what worked, setup drift, your own jet chart, engine health, shock fade and what's due for service.
- **Help & guide** in the app, working offline: guides for each role, a **?** on each page, a **Set up your team** checklist on Home, and **Print / PDF**.
- **Fuel planner:** the fuel to start with, from planned laps and past fuel use.
- **Torsion bar tests:** the rate at a standard arm travel, compression and rebound, the split, and **rate baselines**.
- **Initial measurement** for shocks and bars: later tests show how far they've moved from it. The first test of a shock or bar is ticked as its initial measurement for you.
- **Themes:** pick a theme or make your own. Sidebar sections fold away.

## Improvements
- **Setup sheet quick editing:**
    - Get around with a section bar, put sections in your own order and fold them.
    - Changes are highlighted with what they were, and **Changes only** and a **Before / After** switch show just what matters.
    - Use − and + buttons, and see four-corner values on one row.
- **Shocks on setup sheets:** each corner lists that corner's shocks first, adjuster boxes match the shock's type, and bump rubbers can be recorded.
- **Fill from photo, PDF or Excel**, and table **Paste / import**, read spreadsheets too.
- **Shock dyno test speeds:** test a run at one or more speeds, each with its own readings and curve.
- **Fuel** in US gallons or litres; the team sets the default.
- **One class list:** a car's **Class** picks from **Classes we race** and the shared race classes.
- **Our tracks** starts empty: tracks are added when you race at them or link them from the Directory.
- Every standard field has an **ⓘ** that explains it.
- Page titles and the AI bar stay at the top as you scroll.
- **Calendar quick edit**, sanctioning bodies on series, events and dates, and names for major events.

## Bug fixes
- Long scans, such as a 10-page shock dyno printout, no longer fail to read. They read in the background, and errors say why.
- Opening one record after another no longer carries values from the first into the second.

## Notes
- The Calendar is now under **Team Management**. Drivers and crew can still open it.
- **Classes we race** no longer adds every matching track to **Our tracks**.
